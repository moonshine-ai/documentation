# Hardware

Q: What's actually in the AI Kit box when I get one?
A: It includes M.2 HAT+, Hailo AI module, thermal pad, mounting hardware, and GPIO header.

Q: Does the AI Kit work with any Raspberry Pi or just the Pi 5?
A: The AI Kit is designed specifically for use with Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: What's the performance like on that Hailo chip inside the AI Kit?
A: The Hailo-8L chip delivers thirteen tera-operations per second for neural network inference.

Q: Do I need to buy anything else besides the AI Kit to get it working?
A: You also need a Raspberry Pi 5, a Phillips crosshead screwdriver, and optionally a cooler.

Q: What size is the Hailo AI module? I'm trying to figure out if it fits.
A: The Hailo AI module uses an M.2 2242 form factor.

# Step 1. Mount an Active Cooler (recommended)

Q: Do I need the Active Cooler or can I skip it?
A: It's recommended but optional; skip it if your Pi 5 already has one mounted.

Q: How do I actually attach the cooler to my Pi 5? I've never done this before.
A: Remove pad covers, align push pins with heatsink holes, press down until they click.

Q: I put the cooler on but I'm not sure if it's secure. How do I know it's properly attached?
A: Ensure the push pins are undamaged and securely clipped to the Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: Can I take the cooler off later if I need to?
A: Removing the cooler degrades push pins and thermal pads, potentially causing product damage.

# Step 2. Mount the M.2 HAT+

Q: Which way do I insert the ribbon cable into the Pi? I don't want to break anything.
A: Insert with metallic contact points facing inwards, toward the USB ports.

Q: Do I have to turn off my Pi before connecting anything to the M.2 slot?
A: Always disconnect your Raspberry Pi from power before connecting or disconnecting M.2 devices.

Q: The GPIO header — does it matter which direction I put it on?
A: Orientation doesn't matter as long as all GPIO pins are correctly aligned and inserted.

Q: How do I get the ribbon cable out of the HAT to start with?
A: Slide retaining clips outward from both sides of the PCIe connector, then gently pull.
