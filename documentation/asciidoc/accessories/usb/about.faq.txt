# USB 3 Hub

Q: I've only got one USB port free on my Pi but I need to plug in loads of stuff. What can I do?
A: The Raspberry Pi USB 3 Hub expands one USB-A port into four USB-A ports.

Q: How much power does the hub draw when it's just running off the Pi itself?
A: It draws up to 900 mA, which is 4.5 W, from the upstream USB port.

Q: I want to plug in some power-hungry devices. Can the hub handle that?
A: Yes, plug into the optional USB-C power input to supply up to 15 W externally.

Q: My computer only has USB 2.0 ports. Will the hub still work with it?
A: Yes, the USB 3 Hub is backward compatible with USB 2.0 ports.

Q: How fast is the data transfer on this hub?
A: The hub supports data transfer speeds of up to 5 gigabits per second.

Q: How big is the Raspberry Pi USB 3 Hub? Like what are the actual dimensions?
A: The hub itself is approximately 57.4 mm by 52.4 mm by 18 mm.

Q: What's the total length if you include the cable?
A: Including the cable and USB-A connector, total length is approximately 175 mm.

Q: I just want to plug in a mouse and keyboard. Is the bus power enough for that?
A: Yes, 4.5 W is sufficient for low-power peripherals like most mice and keyboards.

# Flash Drive

Q: Does Raspberry Pi make their own USB stick?
A: Yes, the Raspberry Pi Flash Drive is available in 128 GB and 256 GB capacities.

Q: Can I actually boot an operating system off the Raspberry Pi Flash Drive?
A: Yes, it's designed for running operating systems and is responsive for that task.

Q: I've got a USB 2.0 port on my older computer. Will the flash drive work with it?
A: Yes, the Flash Drive works with USB 3.0 and is backward compatible with USB 2.0.

Q: What's the write speed on the 256 GB version compared to the 128 GB one?
A: The 256 GB version has 150 MB/s sustained sequential write speed versus 75 MB/s.

Q: How small and light is the Raspberry Pi Flash Drive? I want something I can carry around easily.
A: It weighs less than 6 grams and is approximately 42 mm by 12.2 mm.

Q: Can I put it on my keyring?
A: Yes, the compact metal Flash Drive is designed to attach to a keyring or lanyard.

Q: What's the random read performance on the 128 GB version?
A: The 128 GB Flash Drive achieves 16,000 IOPS for 4 kB random reads.

Q: How thick is the flash drive at its thickest point?
A: At its thickest, the Flash Drive is approximately 6.5 mm high.

# Whole Document

Q: Does Raspberry Pi make their own USB accessories or do I have to buy third-party stuff?
A: Raspberry Pi makes their own USB 3 Hub and Flash Drive accessories.

Q: I'm setting up a Pi and need more USB ports and some extra storage. What does Raspberry Pi actually sell for that?
A: Raspberry Pi offers a USB 3 Hub for extra ports and a Flash Drive for storage.

Q: Are these Raspberry Pi USB accessories only for the Pi, or can I use them with a regular computer too?
A: Both accessories work with Raspberry Pi devices and other computers as well.
