# About

Q: What sizes do the official Raspberry Pi SSDs come in?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs come in 256 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB sizes.

Q: Do I need anything special to connect an SSD to my Raspberry Pi?
A: You need a Raspberry Pi 5-compatible M.2 adapter like the M.2 HAT+.

Q: I've heard cheap SSDs can cause problems. Does Raspberry Pi make their own?
A: Yes, Raspberry Pi offers official SSDs tested for compatibility with their models.

Q: Will just any SSD work with a Raspberry Pi, or does it have to be a specific one?
A: Any SSD needs a compatible M.2 adapter; official Raspberry Pi SSDs are tested for compatibility.

# Specifications

Q: What kind of interface do the Raspberry Pi SSDs use?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs use the NVMe 1.4 register interface and command set.

Q: What form factor are the Raspberry Pi SSDs? Like, what size are they physically?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs use the M.2 2230 form factor.

Q: How fast is the 1 TB Raspberry Pi SSD compared to the smaller ones?
A: The 1 TB model reads and writes at 90,000 IOPS, fastest of the three.

Q: What's the read speed on the 512 gig version?
A: The 512 GB SSD has a read speed of 50,000 IOPS using 4 kB random data.

Q: Are these SSDs PCIe compatible or do they use something older?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs are PCIe Gen 3-compliant.

Q: Which Raspberry Pi SSD has the best write speed?
A: Both the 512 GB and 1 TB models top out at 90,000 IOPS write speed.
