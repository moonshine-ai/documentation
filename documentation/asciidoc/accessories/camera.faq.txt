# About the Camera Modules

Q: What's the difference between a standard camera and a NoIR camera?
A: Standard filters out infrared light; NoIR captures both visible and infrared light.

Q: I want to do night vision stuff. Which Raspberry Pi camera should I get?
A: Get a NoIR camera — it captures infrared light, works with infrared lighting.

Q: Can I still buy the original Camera Module 1?
A: No, Camera Module 1 is no longer available from Raspberry Pi.

Q: How many megapixels does the Camera Module 3 have?
A: Camera Module 3 is a 12-megapixel camera built around the Sony IMX708 sensor.

Q: What's the AI Camera actually for?
A: It provides low-latency, high-performance AI capabilities for deploying neural network models.

Q: Does the High Quality Camera come in a NoIR version?
A: No, the High Quality Camera is not available as a NoIR version.

Q: Will these cameras work with my older Raspberry Pi that has a CSI connector?
A: Yes, all Raspberry Pi Camera Modules are compatible with any Pi with a CSI connector.

Q: What makes the Global Shutter Camera different from the others?
A: It captures light from every pixel at once, ideal for fast-motion photography.

# Install a Raspberry Pi camera

Q: I just got a camera module and there's a blue plastic film on the lens. Should I peel that off?
A: Yes, gently peel it off — it's just protective film added during shipping.

Q: I'm worried about static damaging my camera. What should I do before touching it?
A: Earth yourself first by touching a sink tap or using an earthing strap.

Q: What cable do I need to connect a camera to a Raspberry Pi 5?
A: Raspberry Pi 5 uses a mini 22-pin connector, so you need a Standard-Mini camera cable.

Q: Does the Raspberry Pi 4 use the same cable as the Pi 5 for the camera?
A: No, Pi 4 uses the standard 15-pin cable; Pi 5 needs a Standard-Mini cable.

Q: I've got a Pi Zero. Where's the camera connector on it?
A: It's on the short edge to the right, opposite the SD card slot.

Q: Where's the camera connector on a Raspberry Pi 4?
A: It's near the edge closest to you, between the micro HDMI connector and audio jack, labelled CAMERA.

Q: I have a Pi 5 with an Active Cooler. Is there anything special I need to know about connecting the camera?
A: Connect the camera cable before installing the Active Cooler, as it can block access afterward.

Q: How do I open that little flap on the camera connector without breaking it?
A: Gently pull the flap out until it stops, then tilt it slightly away from the connector opening.

Q: Which way do the metal contacts face when I'm inserting the cable?
A: Insert the cable with metallic contacts facing away from the flap.

Q: What software do I need to set up after connecting the camera?
A: Update your OS and then follow the setup instructions for rpicam-apps.

# Global Shutter Camera

Q: Why would I want a global shutter camera instead of a regular one?
A: Global shutter captures all pixels simultaneously, eliminating motion distortion in fast subjects.

Q: What's rolling shutter and why does it cause problems?
A: Rolling shutter scans line-by-line, causing distortion like shimmer in fast-moving objects like propellers.

Q: Can I sync multiple Global Shutter Cameras together?
A: Yes, you can trigger multiple GS cameras simultaneously using the external trigger connection.

Q: What's the shortest exposure time the Global Shutter Camera can do?
A: It can operate with exposure times down to 30 µs, given enough light.

Q: Can I remove the infrared filter from the Global Shutter Camera?
A: Yes, but it's permanent, voids the warranty, and can't be reversed.

# Synchronous Captures

Q: How do I wire up two HQ cameras to sync them?
A: Solder wires to the XVS and GND test points on each camera and connect them together.

Q: Can I sync a Global Shutter Camera with an HQ Camera?
A: No, you can't synchronise a GS Camera and an HQ Camera together.

Q: My synced cameras aren't all starting within a second. What do I do?
A: Edit a libcamera config file and set the camera timeout value to 60000 milliseconds.

Q: Which camera should I start first when doing synchronised recording, the source or the sink?
A: Start the sink first to ensure no frames are missed before the source begins.

Q: I'm using GS cameras in sync mode and the frame counts don't match. Is that normal?
A: Yes, the source records one extra frame — set the sink to record one fewer frame.

# External Trigger on the Global Shutter Camera

Q: How does external triggering work on the Global Shutter Camera?
A: Pulsing the XTR connection captures a frame; framerate equals how often you pulse it.

Q: Can I use a Raspberry Pi Pico to trigger the Global Shutter Camera?
A: Yes, connect a Pico GPIO pin to XTR via a 1.5kΩ resistor to trigger the camera.

Q: What voltage does the external trigger input on the GS Camera expect?
A: XTR is a 1.8V input, so you may need a level shifter or potential divider.

Q: How does the exposure time relate to the trigger pulse?
A: Exposure time equals the low pulse-width time plus an additional 14.26 microseconds.

# IR Filter

Q: Why would I want to remove the IR filter from my camera?
A: To enhance colours in plant or sky photography, or to enable night vision with infrared light.

Q: If I remove the IR filter, can I put it back later?
A: No, removing it is permanent — the adhesive won't survive and it may crack during removal.

Q: Does removing the IR filter void my warranty?
A: Yes, removing the IR filter voids the warranty on the product.

# Hardware specifications

Q: What's the pixel size difference between Camera Module 2 and the Global Shutter Camera?
A: Camera Module 2 has 1.12µm pixels; Global Shutter Camera has much larger 3.45µm pixels.

Q: Which camera module can do the longest exposure, like for night photography?
A: The HQ Camera has the longest maximum exposure time at 670.74 seconds.

Q: Does the Camera Module 1 have adjustable focus?
A: No, Camera Module 1 has fixed focus with a depth of field from approximately 1 metre to infinity.

Q: What video modes does Camera Module 3 support?
A: It supports 2304×1296p56, 2304×1296p30 HDR, and 1536×864p120.
