# External Trigger on the Global Shutter Camera

Q: What's the XTR connection on my Global Shutter camera actually for?
A: It's for triggering the camera externally by pulsing that pin.

Q: I've got two cameras and I want them to sync up. Can I do that with the GS camera?
A: Yes, connect both cameras to the same external trigger pulse for synchronisation.

Q: How do I control the framerate when using external trigger mode?
A: The framerate equals how often you pulse the XTR pin.

Q: If I send a low pulse of 10000 microseconds, what's my actual exposure time?
A: It'll be 10014.26 microseconds, adding a fixed 14.26 microseconds to your pulse.

# Preparation

Q: I need to remove a component to make external triggering work. What do I take off?
A: Remove resistor R11 if transistor Q2 is fitted on your board.

Q: Why do I even need to remove R11? What does it do?
A: R11 connects GP1 to XTR, preventing the camera from working in external trigger mode.

Q: What voltage does XTR take? I don't want to fry my camera.
A: XTR is a 1.8V input, so you may need a level shifter or potential divider.

Q: Can I use a Pico to send the external trigger signal?
A: Yes, connect any Pico GPIO pin to XTR via a 1.5 kΩ resistor.

Q: Why is there a 1.8 kΩ resistor between XTR and ground in the wiring diagram?
A: It reduces the high logic level down to the required 1.8V.

Q: I want to set 30 frames per second with a 6000 microsecond shutter in the Pico code. How does that work?
A: Set PWM frequency to 30 and calculate duty cycle using shutter time and frame length.

# Camera Driver Configuration

Q: Do I need to mess with config.txt for external triggering?
A: Only if you have more than one camera with XTR wired in parallel.

Q: What do I actually change in config.txt for multiple cameras on external trigger?
A: Change camera_auto_detect to 0 and add the dtoverlay equals imx296 always-on line.

Q: I'm using the CAM0 port on a Pi 5. Is there anything extra I need to add?
A: Append comma cam0 without a space to the dtoverlay line for CAM0 port.

Q: I've got both cameras on the same Pi. How many dtoverlay lines do I need?
A: You need two dtoverlay lines, with only one of them ending in comma cam0.

# Starting the Camera

Q: How do I actually enable external trigger mode before running the camera?
A: Run echo 1 piped into sudo tee slash sys module imx296 parameters trigger_mode.

Q: What order do things have to happen — do I start the Pico or the camera first?
A: Run the Pico code first, then start the camera with rpicam-hello afterwards.

Q: Some of my frames are getting dropped. What's causing that?
A: Frames drop when gain and awbgains aren't set, letting AGC and AWB algorithms settle.

Q: Does the shutter value I pass to rpicam-apps actually control the exposure in external trigger mode?
A: No, exposure is controlled by the external trigger pulse, not the shutter value you specify.

Q: Why do I have to specify a fixed shutter when running rpicam-apps with external triggering?
A: It stops the AGC from trying to adjust the camera's shutter speed automatically.
