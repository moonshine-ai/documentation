# About the Camera Modules

Q: What's the difference between the standard camera module and the NoIR version?
A: NoIR versions capture both visible light and infrared, standard captures visible light only.

Q: I'm looking at camera modules and I see Camera Module 1 but I can't find it to buy. Is it discontinued?
A: Yes, Camera Module 1 is no longer available from Raspberry Pi.

Q: How many megapixels is the Camera Module 3?
A: Camera Module 3 is a 12-megapixel camera with four different variants available.

Q: What's special about the Global Shutter Camera? Why would I want it over a regular one?
A: It captures all pixels at once, making it ideal for fast-motion photography.

Q: Does the High Quality Camera come in a NoIR version?
A: No, the High Quality Camera is unavailable as a NoIR version.

Q: I want to use my own lens with a camera module. Which ones support that?
A: The High Quality Camera and Global Shutter Camera both support external lenses via mounts.

Q: Can I use a camera module with any Raspberry Pi?
A: Camera modules work with all Raspberry Pi computers that have CSI connectors.

Q: What's the AI Camera and why would I use it instead of a regular camera module?
A: It offers low-latency AI capabilities and easy neural network model deployment for camera applications.

Q: How many variants does the Camera Module 3 come in exactly?
A: Camera Module 3 comes in four variants: standard and wide FoV in standard and NoIR.

Q: I want to do fast action shots. Which Raspberry Pi camera should I get?
A: The Global Shutter Camera is ideal for fast-motion photography due to its global shutter mechanism.

Q: What's the resolution difference between Camera Module 2 and Camera Module 3?
A: Camera Module 2 is 8 megapixels and Camera Module 3 is 12 megapixels.

A: Check the hardware specifications page to compare all camera module characteristics.
