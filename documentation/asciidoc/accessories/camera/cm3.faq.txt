# Camera Module 3

Q: What's the resolution on the Camera Module 3?
A: It's 4608 by 2592 pixels, totaling twelve megapixels overall.

Q: What sensor does the Camera Module 3 use?
A: It uses the Sony IMX708 sensor for image capture.

Q: Does the Camera Module 3 have autofocus or do I have to do that manually?
A: It has powered autofocus built right in.

Q: I want to do really long exposure shots. How long can the Camera Module 3 expose for?
A: It can record an exposure time of up to 112 seconds.

Q: What versions of the Camera Module 3 are there? I'm not sure which one to buy.
A: There are four variants: standard normal, standard wide, NoIR normal, and NoIR wide.

Q: What's the difference between the standard camera and the NoIR one?
A: Standard filters out infrared; NoIR captures both visible light and infrared.

Q: I want to see in the dark with my Pi camera. Which version do I need?
A: Use a NoIR camera paired with infrared lighting to see in the dark.

Q: There's a square of blue gel in the box. What's that for?
A: It helps the NoIR camera monitor the health of green plants.

Q: I've got a GPS receiver near my Pi and it's acting weird. Could the camera be causing that?
A: The Camera Module 3 may emit RFI that interferes with GPS L1 frequencies.

Q: My GPS stopped working after I plugged in the Camera Module 3. Is there a fix?
A: There are proposed workarounds discussed in a thread on GitHub.
