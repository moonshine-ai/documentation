# Hardware specifications

Q: What's the resolution difference between Camera Module 2 and Camera Module 3?
A: Module 2 has 8 megapixels while Module 3 has 11.9 megapixels.

Q: I want to do astrophotography with really long exposures. Which camera lets me keep the shutter open the longest?
A: The HQ Camera supports the longest maximum exposure time at 670.74 seconds.

Q: Does the original Camera Module 1 have autofocus?
A: No, Camera Module 1 has fixed focus only, not adjustable.

Q: I keep hearing about NoIR cameras. Which ones come in a NoIR version?
A: Camera Modules 1, 2, 3, and 3 Wide all have NoIR versions available.

Q: What's the difference between the regular Camera Module 3 and the Wide version?
A: The Wide version has a wider 102-degree horizontal field of view versus 66 degrees.

Q: How heavy is the HQ Camera compared to the standard modules?
A: The HQ Camera weighs 30.4 grams versus just 3 to 4 grams for standard modules.

Q: I need to film really fast motion. Which camera can shoot the highest frame rate?
A: Camera Module 2 can shoot 640×480 at 206 frames per second.

Q: Can I put my own lens on the HQ Camera?
A: Yes, the HQ Camera supports C/CS-mount or M12-mount lenses.

Q: What sensor does the AI Camera use?
A: The AI Camera uses a Sony IMX500 sensor.

Q: The GS Camera only has 1.58 megapixels — is that right? That seems really low.
A: Yes, the GS Camera's still resolution is just 1.58 megapixels.

Q: Which camera has the biggest pixels, and why does that even matter?
A: The GS Camera has the largest pixels at 3.45 µm × 3.45 µm each.

Q: I want to do close-up macro shots. Which module can focus the closest?
A: The Camera Module 3 Wide can focus from approximately 5 centimetres away.

Q: What's the aperture on Camera Module 3?
A: Camera Module 3 has a focal ratio of F1.8.

Q: Does the GS Camera support interchangeable lenses?
A: Yes, the GS Camera uses a C/CS lens mount for interchangeable lenses.

Q: How big is Camera Module 3 compared to the older modules?
A: Camera Module 3 is slightly thicker at 11.5mm versus 9mm for older modules.
