# Camera Module 2

Q: What sensor is inside the Camera Module 2?
A: It's built around the Sony IMX219 image sensor.

Q: How many megapixels does the Camera Module 2 have?
A: The Camera Module 2 is an eight-megapixel camera.

Q: What's the resolution on the Camera Module 2?
A: It captures images at three thousand two hundred eighty by two thousand four hundred sixty-four pixels.

Q: Does the Camera Module 2 have a fixed focus or can I adjust it?
A: The Camera Module 2 has adjustable focus built in.

Q: How long of an exposure can the Camera Module 2 do?
A: It can record exposures of up to eleven point seven six seconds.

Q: What's the difference between the standard Camera Module 2 and the NoIR version?
A: Standard filters out infrared; NoIR captures both visible and infrared light.

Q: I want to film in the dark. Which version of the Camera Module 2 should I get?
A: Get the NoIR version and pair it with infrared lighting.

Q: I've got the NoIR camera. Can I still use it for regular photos in daylight?
A: Yes, it captures visible light too, just without an infrared filter.

Q: What's that little blue square that comes with the NoIR camera for?
A: It helps monitor the health of green plants when used with the camera.

Q: Where can I find the full technical specs for the Camera Module 2?
A: Check the hardware specifications section in the camera accessories documentation.
