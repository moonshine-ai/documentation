# High Quality Camera

Q: What's the resolution on the High Quality Camera?
A: The High Quality Camera uses a Sony IMX477 sensor with 4056 by 3040 pixels.

Q: What kind of lens mount does the High Quality Camera use?
A: It comes with either an M12-mount or a C/CS-mount for lens compatibility.

Q: How long of an exposure can the High Quality Camera do?
A: The High Quality Camera can record an exposure time of up to 670.74 seconds.

Q: I've got two High Quality Cameras and I want them to shoot at the same time. Is that possible?
A: Yes, you can synchronise multiple High Quality Cameras using an external trigger pulse.

Q: What sensor is inside the High Quality Camera?
A: The High Quality Camera is built around the Sony IMX477 sensor.

# Transmission characteristics

Q: What infrared filter does the High Quality Camera have in it?

Q: I want my High Quality Camera to pick up more infrared light. Can I do that?

Q: If I take out the IR filter, can I put it back later?
