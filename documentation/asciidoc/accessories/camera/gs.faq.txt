# Global Shutter Camera

Q: What's the resolution on the Global Shutter Camera?
A: It captures at 1456 by 1088 pixels, totalling 1.6 megapixels.

Q: What sensor does the Global Shutter Camera use?
A: It's built around the Sony IMX296 image sensor.

Q: Can I use different lenses with the Global Shutter Camera?
A: Yes, it has a C/CS-mount for compatibility with a broad variety of lenses.

Q: I've got multiple cameras and want them all to shoot at exactly the same time. Can I do that?
A: Yes, pulse the external trigger connection to synchronise multiple Global Shutter Cameras.

# Rolling or Global shutter?

Q: What's a rolling shutter and why is it a problem?
A: Rolling shutters scan line-by-line, causing distortion with fast-moving subjects like propellers.

Q: I'm trying to photograph a spinning propeller and it looks all wavy. What's going on?
A: Rolling shutters cause distortion because blades move during the line-by-line scanning process.

Q: So why would I actually want a global shutter over a regular camera?
A: Global shutter captures all pixels at once, eliminating distortion for fast-moving subjects.

Q: How fast can the exposure time get on the Global Shutter Camera?
A: It can go down to 30 microseconds exposure time, given sufficient available light.

Q: I'm doing stereo imaging with two cameras. Why does the global shutter matter there?
A: Synchronised capture prevents brain-confusing mismatches between left and right eye images.

Q: Is the Global Shutter Camera good for machine vision stuff?
A: Yes, its lower resolution and large pixels make real-time image processing much easier.

Q: Why does the Global Shutter Camera have fewer pixels than other cameras? Isn't more always better?
A: More pixels are harder to process in real time, making lower resolution valuable for machine vision.

# Transmission characteristics

Q: Does the Global Shutter Camera have an infrared filter?
A: Yes, it uses a Hoya CM500 infrared filter.

Q: Can I remove the infrared filter to make it more sensitive to IR light?
A: Yes, but removal is permanent and voids your warranty.
