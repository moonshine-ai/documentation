# IR Filter

Q: Why would I even want to remove the IR filter from my camera?
A: It enhances plant photos and enables night vision using infrared light.

Q: I've got the High Quality Camera — does it have an IR filter built in?
A: Yes, both the HQ and Global Shutter cameras include an IR filter.

Q: Wait, if I remove the filter can I just glue it back later?
A: No, the adhesive won't survive removal, making reinstallation difficult or impossible.

Q: Does taking the IR filter off void my warranty?
A: Yes, removing the IR filter voids the warranty on the product entirely.

# Filter Removal

Q: What size hex key do I need to open up the HQ camera?
A: You need a 1.5 mm hex key to unscrew the two lock keys.

Q: The board's really stuck and won't come apart. What can I do?
A: Try isopropyl alcohol or gentle heat around twenty to thirty degrees Celsius.

Q: I'm worried about cracking the filter when I push it out. Any tips?
A: Push only at the very edges using a soft plastic item like a pen top.

Q: My sensor's going to be exposed while I do this. Should I be worried about dust?
A: Work in a clean, dust-free environment to protect the exposed sensor.

Q: Can I put anything over the sensor to keep dust out after removing the filter?
A: You can affix a clear filter like OHP plastic to minimise dust entering.

Q: What order do I put the washers back in when I'm reassembling the camera?
A: Apply the nylon washer first, then the steel washer, then screw down the hex keys.

Q: Do I need to tighten the hex screws really hard when I put it back together?
A: No, as long as washers are fitted correctly, they don't need tightening very much.

Q: I accidentally laid the board flat. Is it okay if the sensor touched the surface?
A: No, you must ensure the sensor doesn't touch the surface during reassembly.

Q: I want to do IR photography of plants and water. Will removing the filter actually help?
A: Yes, filter removal enhances colours in photos of plants, water, and sky.

Q: Does removing the filter work on the Global Shutter Camera too, or just the HQ one?
A: You can remove the filter from both the HQ and GS cameras.
