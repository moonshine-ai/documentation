# C/CS Lenses

Q: What two C/CS lenses do you recommend for the HQ camera?
A: A 6mm wide angle and a 16mm telephoto lens from CGL Electronics.

Q: What's the aperture on the 6mm wide angle lens?
A: The 6mm wide angle lens has a fixed aperture of F1.2.

Q: I want a telephoto lens for my HQ camera. What's the field of view on the 16mm one?
A: The 16mm telephoto gives 22.2 by 16.7 degrees field of view on HQ.

Q: What mount does the 16mm telephoto lens use, C or CS?
A: The 16mm telephoto lens uses a C mount.

Q: How close can I get with these lenses? Like what's the minimum focusing distance?
A: Both the 6mm and 16mm lenses have a minimum object distance of 0.2 metres.

Q: Does the 6mm wide angle work with the GS camera too?
A: Yes, the GS camera has a 45 by 34 degree field of view with that lens.

# M12 Lenses

Q: What M12 lenses do you recommend for the HQ camera?
A: Gaojia Optotech makes the recommended 8mm, 25mm, and fish eye M12 lenses.

Q: I want a really wide angle lens. Is there a fish eye option for the M12 camera?
A: Yes, there's a fish eye M12 lens with a 140 by 102.6 degree field of view.

Q: What resolution is the M12 fish eye lens?
A: The M12 fish eye lens supports up to 15 megapixels resolution.

Q: What's the aperture on the 25mm M12 lens?
A: The 25mm M12 lens has an aperture of F2.4.

# Whole Document

Q: Where do I actually buy these recommended lenses?
A: They should be available from your nearest Raspberry Pi Authorised Reseller.

Q: I've got a GS camera. Can I use M12 lenses on it?
A: No, the GS camera is only available with a C/CS mount, not M12.

Q: What's the difference between the HQ and GS camera versions available?
A: The HQ camera comes in C/CS and M12 mount versions; GS is C/CS only.

Q: I want to buy a lens but I'm not sure if it'll fit my camera. Do these lenses fit both HQ and GS cameras?
A: C/CS lenses fit both HQ and GS cameras; M12 lenses only fit the HQ camera.
