# Mechanical drawings and schematics

Q: Where can I find the mechanical drawings for my Pi camera?
A: They're on the Product Information Portal at pip.raspberrypi.com for each model.

Q: I've got a Camera Module 3 and I want to use it with my Pi Zero case. Will the camera lid fit?
A: No, Camera Module 3 isn't mechanically compatible with the Raspberry Pi Zero camera lid.

Q: Are the mounting holes on Camera Module 3 in the same place as Camera Module 2?
A: Yes, board dimensions and mounting-hole positions are identical between Camera Module 2 and 3.

# Pinout information

Q: I'm looking at my camera connector and I can't figure out which pin is pin 1. How do I find it?
A: Hold your Pi with chip facing up, logo correct; pin 1 is furthest from you, near GPIO.

Q: I've got a Compute Module IO board and I need to find pin 1 on the camera connector. Where is it?
A: On Compute Module IO boards, pin 1 is marked with a small circle or dot.

Q: I'm holding my camera board with the lens facing down. Where's pin 1?
A: With lens down and connector facing right, pin 1 is the pin closest to you.

# Camera connector pinout (15-Pin)

Q: What kind of connector does the 15-pin camera port use? I need to find a compatible part.
A: The 15-pin CSI connector is compatible with the Amphenol SFW15R-2STE1LF.

Q: Which Raspberry Pi boards use the 15-pin camera connector?
A: Flagship Raspberry Pi models prior to Raspberry Pi 5 use the 15-pin CSI connector.

Q: What voltage are the I2C lines pulled up to on the camera connector?
A: The I2C lines SCL and SDA are pulled up to 3.3 volts on the Raspberry Pi board.

Q: What's CAM_IO0 actually used for on the camera connector?
A: CAM_IO0 is typically used as an active-high power enable for the camera module.

Q: Does the 15-pin connector always have both CAM_IO0 and CAM_IO1 pins available?
A: Not always — some products don't include CAM_IO1 on the 15-pin connector.

Q: What pin provides the power supply on the 15-pin camera connector?
A: Pin 15, labeled 3V3, provides the 3.3 volt supply output on the connector.

# Camera connector pinout (22-Pin)

Q: Which Pi boards use the 22-pin camera connector instead of the 15-pin one?
A: The Pi Zero series, Compute Module IO boards, and flagship models since Raspberry Pi 5 use it.

Q: What's the compatible part number for the 22-pin camera connector?
A: The 22-pin CSI connector is compatible with the Amphenol F32Q-1A7H1-11022.

Q: What's the difference between the 15-pin and 22-pin camera connectors in terms of lanes?
A: The 22-pin connector adds D-PHY lanes 2 and 3, giving four data lanes total instead of two.

Q: What pin on the 22-pin connector gives me the 3.3 volt supply?
A: Pin 22, labeled 3V3, provides the 3.3 volt supply output on the 22-pin connector.

Q: I need the schematic for the CSI camera connector. Where do I find it?

Q: Do the GPIO pins on the camera connector work the same way for every camera module?
A: No, the function and direction of GPIO lines depend on the specific camera module used.

Q: I'm building something custom with a Pi camera. Do I need to supply my own pull-up resistors for I2C?
A: No, the I2C lines are already pulled up to 3.3 volts on the Raspberry Pi board itself.
