# About

Q: What exactly is the Raspberry Pi Bumper and what does it do?
A: It's a snap-on silicone cover protecting the bottom and edges of the board.

Q: Does the bumper cover up the mounting holes? I need those for my case.
A: No, the mounting holes remain accessible through the bumper when attached.

Q: Will this bumper work with my Raspberry Pi 4?
A: No, the bumper is only compatible with Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: What's the bumper made of?
A: It's made of silicone material that snaps onto the board.

# Assembly instructions

Q: Do I need to turn off my Pi before putting the bumper on?
A: Yes, turn off your Pi and disconnect the power cable before attaching it.

Q: Wait, do I need to take out my SD card before putting the bumper on?
A: Yes, remove the SD card from the slot before attaching the bumper.

Q: I'm pressing the bumper on but I'm worried about breaking something. How hard should I push?
A: Press gently but firmly, avoiding contact between bumper and board components.

Q: How do I get the bumper off without damaging anything?
A: Gently but firmly peel the bumper away, avoiding contact with board components.

Q: What's the full order of steps to put this thing on?
A: Power off, remove SD card, align, press on, reinsert SD card, reconnect power.
