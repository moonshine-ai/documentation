# Hardware

Q: What's actually in the AI Kit box when you buy one?
A: It includes M.2 HAT+, Hailo AI module, thermal pad, mounting hardware, and GPIO header.

Q: Does the AI Kit work with any Raspberry Pi or just the Pi 5?
A: The AI Kit is designed specifically for use with Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: What's the performance on that Hailo chip thing? Like how fast is it?
A: The Hailo-8L chip delivers thirteen tera-operations per second neural network inference.

Q: Do I need anything else besides the kit itself?
A: You also need a Raspberry Pi 5, a Phillips crosshead screwdriver, and optionally an Active Cooler.

Q: What size is the M.2 module that comes with the kit?
A: The Hailo AI module uses an M.2 2242 form factor.

# Step 1. Mount an Active Cooler (recommended)

Q: Do I have to install the Active Cooler or can I skip it?
A: It's recommended but optional; you can skip to the next step if desired.

Q: How do I actually attach the cooler to my Pi 5?
A: Align push pins to heatsink holes, press gently, then push pins down until they click.

Q: Once I've stuck the cooler on, can I take it back off later?
A: Removing it degrades push pins and thermal pads and can cause product damage.

Q: I need to plug the fan in — where does it connect on the Pi 5?
A: Plug the fan's JST connector into the dedicated fan socket on your Raspberry Pi 5.

# Step 2. Mount the M.2 HAT+

Q: What's the PCIe thing — do I need to know what it is to install this?
A: It's just a high-speed interface connecting the HAT to your Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: Which way does the ribbon cable go in? Like does it matter which direction?
A: Metallic contact points must face inwards, towards the USB ports on your Pi.

Q: Should my Pi be plugged in while I'm connecting the HAT?
A: Always disconnect your Raspberry Pi from power before connecting anything to the M.2 slot.

Q: Does the GPIO stacking header need to go on a certain way?
A: Orientation doesn't matter as long as all GPIO pins are correctly aligned and inserted.
