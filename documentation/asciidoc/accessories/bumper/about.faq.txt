# About

Q: What even is the Raspberry Pi Bumper, like what does it do?
A: It's a snap-on silicone cover protecting the bottom and edges of your board.

Q: Does the bumper cover up the mounting holes? I still need those.
A: No, the mounting holes stay accessible even when the bumper's attached.

Q: Will this bumper work with my Raspberry Pi 4?
A: No, the bumper is only compatible with Raspberry Pi 5.

Q: What's the bumper made of?
A: It's made of silicone.

# Assembly instructions

Q: Do I need to turn off my Pi before putting the bumper on?
A: Yes, turn it off and disconnect the power cable before attaching the bumper.

Q: I'm about to snap the bumper on — do I need to take my SD card out first?
A: Yes, remove the SD card before pressing the board into the bumper.

Q: How hard do I press when putting the bumper on? I don't want to break anything.
A: Press gently but firmly, avoiding contact between the bumper and board components.

Q: How do I take the bumper off without damaging anything?
A: Gently but firmly peel it away, avoiding contact with any board components.

Q: After I put the bumper on, what do I do next to get going again?
A: Reinsert your SD card and then reconnect your Raspberry Pi to power.
