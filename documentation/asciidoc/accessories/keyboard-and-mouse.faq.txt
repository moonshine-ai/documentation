# Getting Started with your Keyboard

Q: What USB ports does the official keyboard have on it?
A: It has three host USB Type A ports for external devices.

Q: I want to plug a mouse into the keyboard instead of the Pi. Can I do that?
A: Yes, the keyboard has three USB ports for mice and other devices.

Q: How does the keyboard connect to the Raspberry Pi?
A: The keyboard connects to the Raspberry Pi via its micro USB port.

Q: Does the keyboard need its own power supply?
A: No, the Raspberry Pi powers and controls the keyboard's USB ports.

# Keyboard Features

Q: There are little lights in the corner of my keyboard. What are they for?
A: They indicate which of the three lock keys are currently enabled.

Q: How do I turn on Num Lock to use number keys?
A: Press the Num Lock key to enable or disable that mode.

Q: I want to type in all capitals. What key do I press?
A: Press Caps Lock to enable typing capital letters automatically.

Q: I've got Caps Lock on but I need one lowercase letter. How do I do that?
A: Hold Shift to type lowercase letters while Caps Lock is enabled.

Q: What does Scroll Lock actually do? I've never used it.
A: It lets you browse web pages and spreadsheets using cursor keys instead.

Q: How do I turn Scroll Lock on?
A: Hold the Fn key and press ScrLk to toggle Scroll Lock on or off.

# Getting Started with your Mouse

Q: How many buttons does the official Raspberry Pi mouse have?
A: The official mouse has three buttons with high-quality micro-switches.

Q: My mouse isn't tracking properly on my desk. What's going on?
A: Featureless surfaces like PVC or acrylic stop optical tracking; use a mousemat.

Q: Does the mouse work on any surface?
A: No, it can't detect movement on featureless surfaces like acrylic or PVC.

Q: What's the scroll wheel for on the mouse?
A: It's for quick scrolling when browsing documents and web pages.

# Connecting it all Together

Q: What's the right way to connect everything together — Pi, keyboard, and mouse?
A: Connect the mouse to the keyboard's USB port and the keyboard to the Pi.

Q: Should I plug the power supply into the keyboard or directly into the Pi?
A: Always connect the power supply directly to the Raspberry Pi, not the keyboard.

Q: What happens if I accidentally plug the power into the keyboard instead of the Pi?
A: The keyboard won't operate correctly if powered that way.

Q: Why is it better to plug the mouse into the keyboard rather than straight into the Pi?
A: It keeps the cable tidy while still allowing left- or right-handed use.

# Whole Document

A: You need the Raspberry Pi itself and a compatible power supply connected to it.

A: Not specified in this section.
