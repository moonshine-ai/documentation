# About

Q: What sizes do the official Raspberry Pi SSDs come in?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs are available in 256 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB.

Q: Do I need anything special to connect an SSD to my Raspberry Pi?
A: You need a Raspberry Pi 5-compatible M.2 adapter like the M.2 HAT+.

Q: Why would I bother getting an official Raspberry Pi SSD instead of just any SSD?
A: Official SSDs are tested for compatibility with Raspberry Pi models and peripherals.

Q: Will any Raspberry Pi work with these SSDs or just certain ones?
A: You need a Raspberry Pi 5-compatible M.2 adapter, implying Raspberry Pi 5 is required.

# Specifications

Q: What's the form factor for the Raspberry Pi SSD? Like will it fit in a standard M.2 slot?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs use the M.2 2230 form factor.

Q: Is the Raspberry Pi SSD PCIe Gen 3 or Gen 4?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs are PCIe Gen 3-compliant.

Q: Which NVMe version do the Raspberry Pi SSDs use?
A: Raspberry Pi SSDs use the NVMe 1.4 register interface and command set.

Q: I'm trying to pick between the 512 GB and 1 TB — is there a speed difference?
A: The 1 TB model reads at 90,000 IOPS versus 50,000 IOPS for the 512 GB.

Q: What's the read speed on the 256 GB Raspberry Pi SSD?
A: The 256 GB SSD has a random read speed of 40,000 IOPS.

Q: Does the bigger SSD actually write faster, or is it the same speed across all sizes?
A: Write speeds vary: 70,000, 90,000, and 90,000 IOPS for 256 GB, 512 GB, and 1 TB respectively.
