# About

Q: What sensors does the Sense HAT actually have on it?
A: It monitors pressure, humidity, temperature, colour, orientation, and movement.

Q: I've got an older Pi with a 40-pin header. Will the Sense HAT work with it?
A: Yes, the Sense HAT works with any Raspberry Pi with a 40-pin GPIO header.

Q: What's that LED grid on the Sense HAT for?
A: The 8x8 RGB LED matrix lets you visualise data from the onboard sensors.

Q: Where did the Sense HAT originally come from? Like why was it made?
A: It was developed for the International Space Station as part of the Astro Pi programme.

Q: Who runs the Astro Pi programme the Sense HAT was made for?
A: The Raspberry Pi Foundation runs it in partnership with the European Space Agency.

Q: What programming language do I use with the Sense HAT?
A: There's an officially supported Python library for the sensors, LED matrix, and joystick.

Q: Does the Sense HAT have any buttons on it?
A: Yes, it has a five-button joystick for users to interact with projects.

Q: I want to do a motion-sensing project. Is the Sense HAT any good for that?
A: Yes, it supports position, motion, orientation, and environmental sensing projects.

Q: What's the Sense HAT actually good for beyond just playing around?
A: It helps with projects requiring position, motion, orientation, or environmental sensing.
