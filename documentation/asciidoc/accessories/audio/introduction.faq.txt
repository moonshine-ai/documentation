# Overview

Q: What's the difference between all these Raspberry Pi audio boards? I don't know which one to get.
A: There are four HAT options, each with different features and audio quality levels.

Q: Do these audio boards work with any Raspberry Pi?
A: They fit any Raspberry Pi with a 40-pin GPIO header.

Q: I want the best possible audio quality. Which board should I get?
A: DAC Pro, DAC Plus, or DigiAMP Plus all support up to full HD audio at 192 kHz.

Q: I'm building something small and I need a microphone built in. Which board has that?
A: The Codec Zero has a built-in microphone and supports up to 96 kHz audio.

Q: What's the max audio quality the Codec Zero supports?
A: Codec Zero supports up to HD audio at 96 kHz sample rate.

# Features at a glance

Q: I want to plug these boards into my existing hi-fi with RCA cables. Which ones have that?
A: DAC Pro and DAC Plus both have line out via phono RCA connectors.

Q: Can any of these boards drive regular passive speakers directly?
A: DigiAMP Plus has stereo speaker outputs for two passive 25-watt speakers.

Q: I just want to plug headphones in. Which boards support that?
A: DAC Pro, DAC Plus, and Codec Zero all have headphone output jacks.

Q: What impedance headphones can these boards handle?
A: The DAC boards can drive headphones up to 80 or 90 ohms impedance.

Q: I want to record audio coming in from my phone. Which board lets me do that?
A: Codec Zero has an aux in input accepting analogue audio up to 1V RMS.

Q: What's the difference between line out and aux out on these boards?
A: Aux out delivers reduced volume up to 1V RMS versus line out's 2V RMS signal.

Q: What's the balanced output connector actually for? I've never used one.
A: It's an XLR connector used in studio setups, high-end hi-fi, and active monitor speakers.

Q: I've got an external microphone. Can any of these boards take that?
A: Codec Zero has a 3.5mm socket for an external electret microphone.

Q: If I plug an external mic into the Codec Zero, does the built-in mic still work?
A: No, the built-in MEMS microphone is automatically disabled when an external mic is connected.

Q: The DigiAMP Plus has speaker terminals — are those for powered speakers or regular ones?
A: Those screw terminals are for passive speakers without built-in amplification.
