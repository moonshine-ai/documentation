# Raspberry Pi DigiAMP+

Q: What's the DigiAMP+ actually for? Like what would I use it for?
A: It connects passive stereo speakers up to 35W for Raspberry Pi hi-fi systems.

Q: How powerful are the speakers I can hook up to this thing?
A: You can connect passive stereo speakers with up to 35 watts variable output.

Q: What power supply do I need for the DigiAMP+?
A: It needs a 12 to 24 volt DC external supply; XP Power VEC65US19 is recommended.

Q: What size is the power connector on the DigiAMP+?
A: The barrel connector is 5.5 millimetres by 2.5 millimetres.

Q: When I first turn it on, why isn't there any sound coming out?
A: The amplifier is muted by default at power-on; software controls the mute state.

Q: What controls the mute button and LED on the DigiAMP+?
A: Software controls both the mute state and LED via Raspberry Pi GPIO pin 22.

Q: Does the DigiAMP+ power my Raspberry Pi or do I need a separate power supply for it?
A: DigiAMP+ delivers 5.1 volts at 2.5 amps to the Raspberry Pi through the GPIO header.

Q: Wait, should I also plug in my Pi's normal power cable when using this?
A: No, do not apply power to the Raspberry Pi's own power input when using DigiAMP+.

Q: What's the P5 connector on the DigiAMP+ for?
A: P5 is an alternative power input for hard-wired installations; observe correct polarity.

Q: What's P8 used for on the DigiAMP+?
A: P8 connects to the TAS5756M's internal GPIO pins one, two, and three.

Q: What chip does the DigiAMP+ use inside?
A: It uses the Texas Instruments TAS5756M PowerDAC chip.
