# Raspberry Pi DAC Pro

Q: What's the DAC Pro and why would I want one for my Pi?
A: It's a high-fidelity digital to analogue converter HAT for Raspberry Pi.

Q: What chip does the DAC Pro use inside?
A: It uses the Texas Instruments PCM5242 chip for conversion.

Q: Does the DAC Pro need its own power supply or does it run off the Pi?
A: It's powered directly by the Raspberry Pi through the GPIO header.

Q: What kind of audio outputs does the DAC Pro actually have?
A: It supports balanced differential output plus phono RCA line-level output.

Q: Does the DAC Pro have a headphone output too, or just line-level?
A: Yes, it includes a dedicated headphone amplifier as well.

# Pinouts

Q: What's coming out of the P1 connector on the DAC Pro?
A: P1 carries analogue audio, GPIO27, mute signal, and left right grounds.

Q: I see P7 and P9 on the board, what are those for?
A: They provide differential output, P7 for left and P9 for right.

Q: Can I power my Pi through the DAC Pro somehow?
A: Yes, P10 is an alternative 5V input that powers the Raspberry Pi.

Q: What's the P6 connector doing on the DAC Pro?
A: P6 carries headphone socket signals including left, right, ground, and detect.

Q: What's the voltage range I can expect from the regular analogue output?
A: The analogue output on P1 delivers zero to two volts RMS.

# Optional XLR Board

Q: What even is an XLR connector? Do I need one?
A: XLR is used in studios and high-end hifi systems, not required.

Q: I've seen the optional XLR board mentioned. What does it actually do?
A: It adds balanced XLR socket outputs above the Pi's USB and Ethernet ports.

Q: Can I use the DAC Pro to drive powered studio monitor speakers?
A: Yes, the XLR board can drive active monitor speakers used on stage.

Q: How does the XLR board connect to the DAC Pro?
A: It connects via a six-pin header exposed on the DAC Pro.

# Whole Document

Q: I want the best audio quality possible from my Pi. Is this the board to get?
A: The DAC Pro is described as Raspberry Pi's highest-fidelity DAC HAT.

Q: What's the difference between the differential output and the regular phono output?
A: Differential output provides zero to four volts RMS versus two volts RMS phono.

Q: I've got a balanced hifi setup at home. Will the DAC Pro work with it?
A: Yes, the DAC Pro supports balanced differential output for such systems.
