# Raspberry Pi Codec Zero

Q: What's the Codec Zero and what does it actually do?
A: It's an audio HAT delivering digital audio signals between Pi and a codec.

Q: Does the Codec Zero have a built-in microphone or do I need to plug one in?
A: It has a built-in MEMS microphone, plus supports external electret microphones too.

Q: What kind of audio quality can I expect from this thing?
A: It supports 24-bit audio at sample rates between 8 and 96 kHz.

Q: I want to plug in RCA cables for audio input. Can this board handle that?
A: Yes, it has stereo auxiliary input via PHONO/RCA connectors.

Q: What happens to the built-in mic when I plug in an external one?
A: The MEMS microphone automatically disables when a mic is inserted.

Q: How loud can the speaker output get on this?
A: The mono speaker driver outputs 1.2 watts at 5 volts into 8 ohms.

Q: Does it have any LEDs or buttons I can use in my project?
A: Yes, there's a green LED, a red LED, and a programmable tactile button.

Q: What GPIO pins are those LEDs and button connected to?
A: Green LED uses GPIO23, red uses GPIO24, and the button uses GPIO27.

Q: What kind of projects is the Codec Zero actually good for?
A: It suits walkie-talkies, smart doorbells, vintage radio hacks, or smart speakers.

Q: I don't want to configure everything manually. Does it support auto-configuration?
A: Yes, it includes an EEPROM that can auto-configure the Linux environment.

# Pinouts

Q: What are those P1 and P2 connectors on the board for?
A: P1 is for auxiliary input and P2 is for auxiliary output via PHONO/RCA sockets.

Q: How do I know which pin is pin one on the P1 connector?
A: Pin 1 on P1 is marked with a square pad.

# Whole Document

Q: I've got a Pi Zero and I want to add audio to it. Is this the right board?
A: Yes, Codec Zero is specifically Pi Zero-sized and adds full audio capability.

Q: Does it support stereo output or just mono?
A: It has stereo auxiliary output but only a mono line-out speaker driver.

Q: Can I do any audio processing on the Codec Zero, like EQ or level control?
A: Yes, it includes a five-band EQ and automatic level control via built-in DSP.
