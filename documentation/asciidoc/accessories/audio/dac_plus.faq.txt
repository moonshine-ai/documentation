# Raspberry Pi DAC+

Q: What's the audio quality like on the DAC plus, like what's the max resolution?
A: The DAC+ supports 24-bit audio at up to 192 kHz sample rate.

Q: Does the DAC plus need its own power supply or does it run off the Pi?
A: It's powered directly through the Raspberry Pi's GPIO header.

Q: What kind of output connectors does the DAC plus have?
A: It has phono connectors for analogue audio output.

Q: I've got a DAC plus and want to plug in headphones. Does it support that?
A: Yes, it has a dedicated headphone amplifier built in.

Q: What chip does the DAC plus use inside?
A: It uses a Texas Instruments PCM5122 chip for analogue audio delivery.

# Pinouts

Q: What's the P1 connector on the DAC plus actually for?
A: P1 carries analogue audio out, GPIO27, mute signal, and ground connections.

Q: What's the voltage range on the analogue output from P1?
A: The analogue output on P1 is zero to two volts RMS.

Q: I'm trying to wire up headphones to the P6 connector. Which pin is left audio?
A: Pin one on P6 is the left channel audio signal.

Q: What does pin five on the P6 headphone connector do?
A: Pin five on P6 is the detect signal for headphone detection.

# Whole Document

Q: So the DAC plus sits on top of the Pi, right? How does it connect?
A: It connects via the Raspberry Pi GPIO header as a HAT.

A: Yes, it delivers high-resolution 24-bit 192 kHz analogue audio output.
