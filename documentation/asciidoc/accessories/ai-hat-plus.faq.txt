# Whole Document

Q: What even is an AI HAT and do I need one for my Raspberry Pi?
A: It's an add-on board with a Hailo NPU for local hardware-accelerated AI.

Q: Does the AI HAT work with any Raspberry Pi or just the Pi 5?
A: AI HATs are designed specifically for Raspberry Pi 5 only.

Q: I don't want my data going to the cloud. Can the AI HAT keep everything local?
A: Yes, the Hailo NPU processes AI models locally, keeping your data private.

Q: What's the difference between the AI HAT Plus and the AI HAT Plus 2?
A: AI HAT+ 2 has its own 8GB memory and supports LLMs and VLMs; AI HAT+ doesn't.

Q: I've got an old AI Kit lying around. Is that the same as an AI HAT?
A: AI Kit matches Hailo-8L AI HAT+ specs but is no longer in production.

---

# How it works

Q: Do I have to do anything special to get Raspberry Pi OS to recognize the AI HAT?
A: No, Raspberry Pi OS automatically detects the accelerator when the HAT is connected.

Q: Can I use my Pi camera with the AI HAT for things like object detection?
A: Yes, rpicam-apps and Picamera2 automatically use the Hailo NPU for supported tasks.

Q: I just plugged in my AI HAT. Is that all I need to start running AI models?
A: No, you also need to install additional software and supported AI models separately.

Q: Does the AI HAT work with Picamera2?
A: Yes, Picamera2 can automatically use the Hailo NPU for supported AI computing tasks.

---

# Models

Q: Can I run a chatbot or language model locally on the AI HAT Plus?
A: No, LLM support is only available on the AI HAT+ 2, not the original AI HAT+.

Q: What's the memory situation with the AI HAT Plus? Does it have its own RAM?
A: No, AI HAT+ uses Raspberry Pi 5's memory; only AI HAT+ 2 has its own 8GB.

Q: How big of a language model can the AI HAT Plus 2 handle?
A: It can handle LLMs and VLMs up to approximately six billion parameters.

Q: I want to do robotics stuff. Which AI HAT should I get?
A: Object detection and robotics are listed use cases for the AI HAT+.

Q: What chip is inside the AI HAT Plus 2?
A: The AI HAT+ 2 uses the Hailo-10H neural processing unit running at 40 TOPS.

---

# Performance (TOPS) variants

Q: What does TOPS even mean? Is bigger always better?
A: TOPS means tera-operations per second, measuring how fast the NPU runs inference.

Q: What's the difference between the 13 TOPS and 26 TOPS version of the AI HAT Plus?
A: The 26 TOPS version supports larger networks, higher throughput, and parallel AI models.

Q: I just want something similar to the old AI Kit. Which variant should I get?
A: The 13 TOPS AI HAT+, built around the Hailo-8L, performs similarly to the AI Kit.

Q: Can the AI HAT Plus 2 do everything the AI HAT Plus can, plus more?
A: Yes, AI HAT+ 2 supports all AI HAT+ workloads plus local LLMs and VLMs.

# Physical specifications

Q: Are the AI HAT Plus and AI HAT Plus 2 the same size?
A: Yes, both are approximately 66mm by 56.5mm, the same physical size.

Q: What's the main visual difference between the two AI HAT models?
A: AI HAT+ 2 visually has SDRAM chips on it; AI HAT+ does not.

Q: Does the AI HAT Plus 2 come with a heatsink included?
A: Yes, AI HAT+ 2 comes with an additional heatsink included in the box.

# Hardware assembly

Q: What screwdriver do I need to install the AI HAT?
A: You need a Phillips crosshead screwdriver to attach the spacers and mount the HAT.

Q: What comes in the box with the AI HAT?
A: Spacers, long and short screws, a GPIO stacking header, and a ribbon cable are included.

Q: Do I need to buy anything extra to install my AI HAT?
A: You need a Raspberry Pi 5 and a Phillips screwdriver; an Active Cooler is recommended.

Q: Should I turn my Pi off before putting the AI HAT on?
A: Yes, ensure your Raspberry Pi 5 is disconnected from power before you begin.

---

# Step 1. Mount an Active Cooler (recommended)

Q: How do I attach the Active Cooler? Do I need to peel anything off first?
A: Remove protective paper from thermal pads, align push pins, press down until they click.

Q: Once I've stuck the Active Cooler on, can I take it off again later?
A: It's not recommended, as removal degrades push pins and thermal pads, risking damage.

Q: How does the fan connect to the Pi after I mount the cooler?
A: Plug the fan's JST connector into the fan socket on your Raspberry Pi 5.

---

# Step 2. Mount the AI HAT+ 2 heatsink (recommended)

Q: Do I have to put the heatsink on the AI HAT Plus 2 or is it optional?
A: It's optional but strongly recommended, especially for intensive AI workloads or benchmarks.

Q: Why would I bother with the heatsink on the AI HAT Plus 2?
A: Without it, the AI HAT+ 2 may slow down due to overheating during intensive workloads.

Q: Which chips does the AI HAT Plus 2 heatsink actually touch?
A: Its thermal pads align with the power regulator, Hailo NPU, and the SDRAM chip.

---

# Step 3. Mount the AI HAT

Q: How does the AI HAT actually connect to the Pi? Is it just the GPIO pins?
A: It connects via the Pi 5's PCIe port using a ribbon cable, not just GPIO pins.

Q: Does it matter which way around I put the GPIO stacking header?
A: Orientation doesn't matter as long as all GPIO pins are correctly aligned and inserted.

Q: Which way does the ribbon cable go into the PCIe connector on the Pi?
A: Insert it with metallic contact points facing inwards, toward the USB ports.

Q: I'm mounting the AI HAT now. Do the longer or shorter screws go into the Pi first?
A: Use the four longer screws to attach spacers to the Pi, then shorter screws for the HAT.
