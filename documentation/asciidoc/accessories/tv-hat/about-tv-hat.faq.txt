# About

Q: What's this TV HAT thing and what does it actually do?
A: It lets you receive, view, or stream digital terrestrial TV on your Pi.

Q: Can I use the TV HAT to stream TV to other devices on my network?
A: Yes, you can use it as a TV server to stream to other network devices.

Q: What Pi do I need if I want to watch TV directly on the Pi itself?
A: Raspberry Pi 2 or later is recommended for viewing TV on the Pi.

Q: What tuner chip is in the TV HAT?
A: It uses a Sony CXD2880 TV tuner chip.

Q: What channel bandwidths does DVB-T2 support on this thing?
A: DVB-T2 supports 1.7, 5, 6, 7, and 8 MHz channel bandwidths.

# About DVB-T

Q: Does this work in the US? I'm in North America.
A: No, the TV HAT doesn't support ATSC, the North American digital TV standard.

Q: What is DVB-T even? I keep seeing it mentioned.
A: It's the European consortium standard for digital terrestrial television broadcast transmission.

Q: My country doesn't use DVB-T. Will the HAT still work for me?
A: Only DVB-T and DVB-T2 are supported, so it depends on your country's standard.

# Install

Q: How do I actually install the software to get TV working?
A: Run sudo apt update then sudo apt install tvheadend in the terminal.

Q: I've got the HAT physically connected. Which way does it go on?
A: Point the aerial adaptor away from the USB ports, then press gently onto GPIO pins.

Q: I set up tvheadend but now I can't remember what password I chose. What do I do?
A: Not specified in this section.

Q: How do I connect to tvheadend from another computer?
A: Open a browser and go to http://raspberrypi.local:9981/extjs.html on your network.

Q: I don't know what to put in the allowed network field during setup. Can I just leave it?
A: Leave Allowed Network blank and enter an asterisk in username and password fields.

Q: I finished scanning and it found muxes but I don't see any channels yet. What's next?
A: In Service Mapping, tick all three boxes to map services and create tags.

Q: The video in my browser is really jerky. Is that normal?
A: Yes, browser playback can be jerky; using a local media player is recommended instead.

Q: How do I watch a channel in VLC instead of the browser?
A: Click the i icon on a channel to get a downloadable stream file for VLC.

Q: I don't know which TV transmitter to pick during setup. How do I find out?
A: Use the Freeview website and enter your postcode to find your best local transmitter.

Q: Can the TV HAT tune into multiple channels at once?
A: No, it can only decode one mux at a time, though each mux contains several channels.

Q: Is there an app I can use on my phone to watch TV from my Pi?
A: Yes, tvheadend supports numerous apps including TvhClient for iOS.

# Whole Document

Q: I just got a TV HAT, what's the minimum Pi I should use?
A: Any 40-pin Pi works as a server, but Pi 2 or later is best for local viewing.

Q: Do I need a TV aerial to use this thing?
A: Yes, you connect your TV aerial cable to the HAT's aerial adaptor.

Q: What operating system do I need on my Pi for this to work?
A: You need the newest version of Raspberry Pi OS installed on your Pi.
