# Connecting it all Together

Q: Where should I plug in the power supply, into the Pi or the keyboard?
A: Always connect the power supply directly to the Raspberry Pi itself.

Q: Does it matter which order I connect things? Like can I just plug power into the keyboard hub?
A: No, plugging power into the keyboard causes the keyboard to not work correctly.

Q: I've got the official keyboard with the built-in hub — what's that hub actually useful for?
A: The keyboard hub gives you easy access to plug in USB drives.

Q: Why is the mouse cable long on the official mouse? Is there a reason for that?
A: The cable is long enough to use the mouse left- or right-handed comfortably.

Q: I'm setting up my Pi with the official keyboard and mouse. What's the recommended way to connect everything?
A: Connect power to the Pi, keyboard to the Pi, and mouse through the keyboard hub.

Q: Wait, so the keyboard goes into the Pi, not the other way around? I'm confused about the setup.
A: Yes, the keyboard connects to the Raspberry Pi, not the Pi to the keyboard.

Q: I plugged power into the keyboard hub and now my keyboard isn't working right. What did I do wrong?
A: Power must go to the Pi directly; powering via the keyboard causes incorrect keyboard operation.
