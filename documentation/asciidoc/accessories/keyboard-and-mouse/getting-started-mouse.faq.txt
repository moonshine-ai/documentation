# Getting Started with your Mouse

Q: How many buttons does the official Raspberry Pi mouse have?
A: The official Raspberry Pi mouse has three buttons with high-quality micro-switches.

Q: What's the scroll wheel actually for on the Pi mouse?
A: The wheel is for quick scrolling when browsing documents and web pages.

Q: My mouse isn't moving the cursor properly. What could be wrong?
A: It may be on a featureless surface like acrylic where it can't detect movement.

Q: Do I need a mouse mat for the Raspberry Pi mouse?
A: Only if your surface is featureless, like PVC or acrylic table tops.

Q: The mouse cursor is just frozen and won't move. Any ideas?
A: Place the mouse on a flat, stable surface or use a mouse mat.

Q: Why does my mouse work fine on my desk but not on my plastic table?
A: Featureless surfaces like PVC or acrylic prevent the mouse from detecting movement.

Q: What kind of switches does the official Pi mouse use?
A: The official mouse uses high-quality micro-switches for its three buttons.
