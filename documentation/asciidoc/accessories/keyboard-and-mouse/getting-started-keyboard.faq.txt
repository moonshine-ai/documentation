# Getting Started with your Keyboard

Q: What USB ports are on the official Raspberry Pi keyboard?
A: It has three host USB Type A ports for external devices.

Q: I've got the Raspberry Pi keyboard, how does it connect to my Pi?
A: Connect it using the keyboard's micro USB port to your Raspberry Pi.

Q: Can I plug a mouse into the keyboard itself instead of directly into the Pi?
A: Yes, the three USB ports on the keyboard support mice and other devices.

Q: Who's actually powering the USB ports on the keyboard, is that separate?
A: The Raspberry Pi controls and powers the keyboard's three USB ports via a built-in hub.

# Keyboard Features

Q: There are little lights in the corner of my keyboard, what do they mean?
A: They indicate which of the three lock keys are currently enabled.

Q: I accidentally hit something and now my letters are typing numbers. What happened?
A: You pressed Num Lock, enabling the red number keys on the letter keys.

Q: How do I turn off Caps Lock once I've turned it on?
A: Just press the Caps Lock key again to toggle it off.

Q: What's Scroll Lock even for? I never use it.
A: It lets you browse web pages and spreadsheets using cursor keys without a mouse.

Q: How do I turn on Scroll Lock? Pressing the button doesn't seem to do anything.
A: Hold the Fn key while pressing the ScrLk key to toggle Scroll Lock.

Q: I want to type a lowercase letter while Caps Lock is on. Can I do that?
A: Yes, hold the Shift key to type lowercase letters while Caps Lock is on.
