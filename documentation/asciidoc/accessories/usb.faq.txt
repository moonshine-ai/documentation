# USB 3 Hub

Q: I've only got one USB port free on my Pi. Can I plug a hub in and get more ports?
A: Yes, the USB 3 Hub turns one USB-A port into four USB-A ports.

Q: Will my old USB 2 devices work if I plug them into this hub?
A: Yes, the hub is backward compatible with USB 2.0 devices.

Q: I want to plug in a bunch of stuff but I'm worried about power. How much can the hub actually handle?
A: Bus-powered, it draws up to 900 mA shared across all four downstream ports.

Q: My keyboard and mouse aren't getting enough power through the hub. Is there something I can do?
A: Connect external power through the optional USB-C input, supplying up to 15 W.

Q: How fast is data transfer through the USB 3 Hub?
A: It supports data transfer speeds of up to 5 gigabits per second.

Q: How big is the hub? Like will it be huge and take up loads of desk space?
A: The hub itself is approximately 57.4 mm by 52.4 mm by 18 mm.

Q: Does the hub come with a cable attached or do I need to bring my own?
A: It has an 8 cm captive upstream USB 3.0 Type-A cable already attached.

Q: I want to power some more demanding devices through the hub. How much power can I get with external power connected?
A: With self-powered USB-C input, it supplies up to 5 V at 3 A, totalling 15 W.

# Flash Drive

Q: What sizes does the Raspberry Pi Flash Drive come in?
A: It's available in either 128 GB or 256 GB storage capacities.

Q: Can I actually boot my Pi from this flash drive?
A: Yes, it's designed for responsive performance for booting operating systems.

Q: I want to carry this thing on my keys. Is it small enough for that?
A: Yes, it has a compact metal design that can attach to a keyring or lanyard.

Q: Will this flash drive work on my older computer that only has USB 2?
A: Yes, it's backward compatible with USB 2.0 ports.

Q: Does the flash drive keep my files when I unplug it?
A: Yes, it uses non-volatile flash memory to retain data when disconnected.

Q: Is there much difference in speed between the 128 gig and 256 gig versions?
A: Yes, the 256 GB version is notably faster, especially for sequential write speeds.

Q: How heavy is this flash drive? I don't want something bulky.
A: It weighs less than 6 grams, making it very lightweight and travel-friendly.

Q: What's the sequential write speed on the bigger flash drive?
A: The 256 GB version has a sustained sequential write speed of 150 MB/s.
