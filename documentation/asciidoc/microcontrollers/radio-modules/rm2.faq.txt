# RM2 key features

Q: What kind of Wi-Fi does the RM2 support?
A: RM2 supports Wi-Fi 4, single band 2.4 GHz, standard 802.11b/g/n.

Q: Does the RM2 do Bluetooth? Like what version?
A: Yes, RM2 supports Bluetooth 5.2, both Classic and Low Energy BLE.

Q: I've got a Pico W project — will the RM2 work with the same software?
A: Yes, RM2 is fully software compatible with Pico W and Pico 2 W SDK.

Q: What chip is inside the RM2?
A: RM2 uses the Infineon CYW43439 combo chip in a single-board configuration.

Q: Does the RM2 have its own antenna or do I need to add one?
A: RM2 has a shared 2.4 GHz antenna for both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth built in.

Q: How fast can the RM2 actually go for Wi-Fi?
A: RM2 supports 20 MHz channels with data rates up to 96 Mbps PHY rate.

Q: What's the operating temperature range for the RM2?
A: RM2 operates across a wide range from negative 30 to positive 70 degrees Celsius.

Q: How much power does the RM2 draw when it's transmitting?
A: Transmit active rate at MCS7 and 16 dBm draws approximately 271 milliamps.

Q: I heard the RM2 has some extra GPIO pins. How many are there?
A: RM2 provides three host-controlled GPIOs for added input and output capabilities.

Q: What does the RM2 use to connect to a host board, like what interface?
A: RM2 uses a low-pin-count gSPI host interface for simplified integration.

# Technical specifications

Q: How big is the RM2? I need to know if it'll fit my board.
A: RM2 is approximately 14.5 by 16.5 millimetres with a thickness of about 2.55 mm.

Q: How many pins does the RM2 have for soldering it down?
A: RM2 has 21 edge-plated castellated pins for secure soldering to other boards.

Q: What material is the RM2 PCB made from?
A: RM2's PCB is made from flame retardant FR4 material.

Q: What's the pin pitch on the RM2 contacts?
A: RM2 uses 1.5 mm pitch gold-plated contacts for reliable electrical connectivity.

# Compliance

Q: Is the RM2 already certified so I don't have to do all my own radio testing?
A: Yes, RM2 is pre-certified and fully tested by Raspberry Pi in key global markets.

Q: Can I use the RM2's certification for my own product in Europe without extra testing?
A: Yes, full modular approval in the EU means minimal additional testing is required.

Q: What countries is the RM2 approved for use in?
A: RM2 is approved in the EU, UK, USA, Canada, India, and Malaysia.

Q: I'm building something with the RM2 in India — do I still need my own approval?
A: Yes, in India modular approval doesn't extend to host devices, so separate approval is needed.

Q: Where do I find the actual test reports and certifications for the RM2?
A: Test reports and certifications are available through the Raspberry Pi Product Information Portal.

Q: I want to sell my RM2-based product somewhere not on the approved list. Who do I contact?
A: Contact the Global Market Access team at gma@raspberrypi.com for support.

# Whole Document

Q: What even is the RM2? Like what is it for?
A: RM2 is a compact self-contained Wi-Fi and Bluetooth radio module from Raspberry Pi.

Q: Is the RM2 already tuned or do I have to do RF tuning myself on my production line?
A: RM2 is fully tested and tuned by Raspberry Pi, requiring no RF tuning on your line.

Q: Does the RM2 need separate antennas for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth?
A: No, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth share a single 2.4 GHz antenna on the RM2.
