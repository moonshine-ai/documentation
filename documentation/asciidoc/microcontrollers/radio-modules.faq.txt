# Whole Document

Q: What's the difference between RM1 and RM2? Can I buy either of them?
A: Only RM2 is available to buy; RM1 is built into Pi 4, 5, and Compute Modules.

Q: I've got a Raspberry Pi 4 — does it already have a radio module built in?
A: Yes, Raspberry Pi 4 has RM1 built in already.

Q: I'm building a project with a Pi microcontroller and I need wireless. Which radio module should I look at?
A: RM2 is designed to add wireless connectivity to Raspberry Pi microcontrollers.

# RM2 key features

Q: What kind of Wi-Fi does the RM2 support? Is it dual band?
A: RM2 supports single band 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 4, so no dual band.

Q: Does the RM2 do Bluetooth? Like what version?
A: Yes, RM2 supports Bluetooth 5.2, including Classic and Bluetooth Low Energy.

Q: What chip is inside the RM2?
A: RM2 uses the Infineon CYW43439 combo chip inside.

Q: Will RM2 work with my Pico W code? I don't want to rewrite everything.
A: RM2 is fully software compatible with Pico W and Pico 2 W SDK.

Q: Does RM2 have any extra GPIO pins I can use?
A: Yes, RM2 includes three host-controlled GPIOs for added I/O capabilities.

Q: How fast is the RM2's Wi-Fi? Like what's the max speed?
A: RM2 supports data rates up to 96 Mbps on 20 MHz channels.

Q: I'm worried about power draw. How much current does RM2 use when it's just sitting there in power save mode?
A: In IEEE Power Save mode, RM2 draws an average of 1.19 mA.

Q: Can RM2 handle cold temperatures? I'm putting it somewhere that gets pretty cold.
A: RM2 operates down to negative 30 degrees Celsius, so cold is fine.

Q: Does RM2 use separate antennas for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth or just one?
A: RM2 uses a single shared 2.4 GHz antenna for both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

# Technical specifications

Q: How big is the RM2? I need to know if it'll fit in my enclosure.
A: RM2 is approximately 14.5 by 16.5 mm with a thickness of about 2.55 mm.

Q: How does RM2 connect to a board — like does it solder on or plug in?
A: RM2 has 21 castellated pins and 1.5 mm pitch gold-plated contacts for soldering.

Q: What material is the RM2 circuit board made from?
A: RM2's PCB is made from flame retardant FR4 material.

# Compliance

Q: Do I need to do my own radio certification if I'm selling a product that uses RM2 in the US?
A: No, in the USA you can use RM2's existing full modular approval certification.

Q: I'm making a product with RM2 and selling it in India. Do I still need my own certification?
A: Yes, in India modular approval doesn't extend to host devices; you need separate approval.

Q: Where can I find the test reports and certifications for RM2?
A: You can access them through Raspberry Pi's Product Information Portal at pip.raspberrypi.com.

Q: I want to sell something with RM2 in a country that's not on the approved list. Who do I talk to?
A: Contact the Global Market Access team at gma@raspberrypi.com for support.

Q: Does RM2 need extra RF tuning before I can use it in my product?
A: No, RM2 is fully tested and tuned by Raspberry Pi; no further RF tuning needed.
