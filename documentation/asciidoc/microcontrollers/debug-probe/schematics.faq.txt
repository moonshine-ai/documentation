# Schematics

Q: Where can I find the schematics for the Debug Probe?
A: Schematics are available as a PDF at pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008194-DS.

Q: Is there a mechanical drawing for the Debug Probe? I need the dimensions.
A: Yes, a mechanical diagram PDF is available at pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008192-DS.

Q: I'm looking at the schematic and I see these TP labels everywhere. What are those?
A: TP stands for test points, and their locations are shown in a diagram.

Q: I need to find the test points on my actual Debug Probe board. Is there a diagram showing where they are?
A: Yes, a diagram shows the physical locations of all test points on the board.
