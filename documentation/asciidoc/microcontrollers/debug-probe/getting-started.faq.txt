# Getting started

Q: How do I connect the Debug Probe to my Pico H? What ports do I use?
A: Connect the "D" port to SWD and "U" port to UART pins.

Q: I've got a regular Pico, not the H version. Can I still use the Debug Probe with it?
A: Yes, solder a male connector to SWCLK, GND, and SWDIO header pins.

Q: What's the difference between the D port and the U port on the Debug Probe?
A: The D port is for SWD debugging and U port handles serial UART communication.

Q: I'm connecting the serial pins and I want to make sure I've got them the right way round. Does RX go to RX?
A: No, Debug Probe RX connects to Pico TX, and TX connects to RX.

Q: My Pico doesn't have that JST connector. What cable do I need to hook it up?
A: Use the included three-pin JST-SH to 0.1-inch female header cable instead.

Q: Okay I've got a non-H Pico and I'm trying to wire the SWD pins. Which wire colour goes where?
A: Orange is SC, black is GND, and yellow is SD on the Debug Probe.

Q: Do I need to solder anything if I have the Pico H version?
A: No soldering needed; Pico H has a built-in JST-SH connector for SWD.
