# Create a Raspberry Pi ID

Q: How do I make a Raspberry Pi ID account?
A: Go to id.raspberrypi.com, enter email and password, then click Sign up.

Q: I signed up but nothing happened after. Do I need to do anything else?
A: Check your email for a verification message and click the Verify email button.

Q: I've got my account now. How do I log into Raspberry Pi services with it?
A: Use the Sign in with Raspberry Pi ID button on any Raspberry Pi service.

Q: Do I need an account to use Raspberry Pi services, or can I just dive in?
A: Yes, you must create a Raspberry Pi ID first to use Raspberry Pi services.

# Enable Two-factor authentication

Q: What even is two-factor authentication? Do I need it?
A: 2FA adds an extra login layer beyond your password, using something you have or are.

Q: I want to set up two-factor authentication. What app should I use on my phone?
A: Any TOTP app works, like Authy or Google Authenticator; install one first.

Q: Okay I've got the app installed. Now how do I actually turn on 2FA for my account?
A: Sign in to id.raspberrypi.com and click the Two-factor authentication option.

Q: I've scanned the QR code. What do I do next to finish setting up 2FA?
A: Enter the six-digit TOTP from your 2FA app to register it with your account.

Q: What's that recovery code for? Can I just ignore it?
A: It's the only way to bypass 2FA if you lose your phone; store it safely.

Q: I lost my phone and now I can't log in with 2FA. What do I do?
A: Use the recovery code you saved during 2FA setup to bypass authentication.

Q: I've got an iPhone. Is there anything special I can do instead of using a separate app?
A: On iPhone or Mac, right-click the QR code for iCloud Keychain verification code setup.

Q: Can I turn off two-factor authentication later if I change my mind?
A: Yes, you can disable two-factor authentication anytime at id.raspberrypi.com.
