# Create a Raspberry Pi ID

Q: How do I make a Raspberry Pi ID account?
A: Go to id.raspberrypi.com, enter email and password, then click Sign up.

Q: I signed up but I'm not sure what to do next. Do I need to verify my email or anything?
A: Yes, click the Verify email button in the verification email you receive.

Q: Do I need an account to use Raspberry Pi services?
A: Yes, you must create a Raspberry Pi ID to use Raspberry Pi services.

Q: I made my account, now how do I actually sign in to Raspberry Pi stuff?
A: Use the Sign in with Raspberry Pi ID button on Raspberry Pi services.

Q: What website do I go to to create my Raspberry Pi ID?
A: Go to id.raspberrypi.com in your browser to create your account.

Q: I didn't get a verification email after signing up. What should've happened?
A: You should receive a verification email after creating your account.
