# Introduction

Q: What's the Bookshelf app actually for on Raspberry Pi OS?
A: It gives you access to books and magazines published by Raspberry Pi Press.

Q: I see some books have a little cloud icon and some have a padlock. What's the difference?
A: Cloud icons are free downloads; padlocks mean the title requires contributor access.

Q: I'm not on the latest OS version. Does Bookshelf still work for me?
A: Contributor-only titles are only available since Raspberry Pi OS Trixie.

Q: I installed Raspberry Pi OS Lite and I can't find Bookshelf anywhere. Why?
A: Bookshelf is only included in the Desktop and Full versions of Raspberry Pi OS.

Q: Can anyone download the free titles or do I need an account?
A: Free titles marked with the cloud icon can be downloaded by anyone.

# Become a contributor (Raspberry Pi OS Trixie and later)

Q: I want access to the locked books. What do I need to do?
A: Sign in with a Raspberry Pi ID linked to a subscription or monthly contribution.

Q: How do I actually set up the monthly contribution thing inside Bookshelf?
A: Select Contribute in the bottom left to open the contributor page on the website.

Q: I already have a Raspberry Pi ID. Do I need to make a new one to contribute?
A: No, just sign in with your existing username and password during the setup process.

Q: I got a new Raspberry Pi. Do I have to pay again to get my contributor books back?
A: Just sign in with your Raspberry Pi ID again and re-download your favourite titles.

Q: After I sign up as a contributor, how do I know it actually worked?
A: The Contribute button disappears and all titles become available to download.

# Download titles

Q: How do I actually open one of the books once I find it in Bookshelf?
A: Double-click any available title to download it as a PDF and open it.

Q: I cancelled my subscription. Do I lose all the books I already downloaded?
A: No, you keep any titles already downloaded even after stopping your contribution.

Q: Where does Bookshelf actually save the PDF files on my Pi?
A: PDFs are stored in the Bookshelf folder inside your home directory.

# Whole Document

Q: What even is Bookshelf? I've never heard of it before.
A: It's a Raspberry Pi OS app for accessing Raspberry Pi Press books and magazines.

Q: So do I need to pay anything to use Bookshelf at all?
A: No, free titles are available to anyone; contributor-only titles require a subscription or contribution.
