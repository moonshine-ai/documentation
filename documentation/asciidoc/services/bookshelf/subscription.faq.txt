# Become a contributor (Raspberry Pi OS Trixie and later)

Q: How do I get access to the contributor-only titles in Bookshelf?
A: Sign in with a Raspberry Pi ID linked to a magazine subscription or contribution.

Q: I've got a magazine subscription. Do I get the extra Bookshelf titles automatically or do I have to do something?
A: Sign in to Bookshelf with a Raspberry Pi ID associated with your subscription email.

Q: I don't have a Raspberry Pi ID yet. Can I make one when I'm setting up my contribution?
A: Yes, you can create a Raspberry Pi ID during the monthly contribution sign-up process.

Q: I just signed up for the monthly contribution. What do I do next to unlock the titles?
A: Select "Sign in to Bookshelf" to store your ID and unlock all available titles.

Q: How do I know if the contributor thing worked? Is there something that changes on screen?
A: The "Contribute..." button disappears and all titles become available for download.

Q: I've got two Raspberry Pis. Can I use my contributor account on both of them?
A: Yes, you can sign in and access titles from any number of devices.

Q: I just reflashed my SD card and now I can't see my titles. What do I do?
A: Sign in with your Raspberry Pi ID again and re-download your favourite titles.

# Download titles

Q: How do I actually download one of the magazines? Do I just click on it?
A: Double-click any available title to download it as a PDF and open it.

Q: Where does Bookshelf save the PDFs on my Pi?
A: PDFs are stored in the Bookshelf folder inside your home directory.

Q: I'm thinking of cancelling my monthly contribution. Will I lose all the issues I've already downloaded?
A: No, you keep any titles already downloaded even after stopping your contribution.

Q: If I cancel my subscription, can I still open the PDFs I already have through Bookshelf?
A: Yes, downloaded PDFs remain accessible through Bookshelf after cancellation.

# Whole Document

Q: So what even is Bookshelf on Raspberry Pi? Is it just for reading the official magazine?
A: Bookshelf lets contributors download and read magazine titles as PDFs on their Pi.

Q: Do I need to pay anything to get the extra titles, or is there a free option?
A: You need either a print magazine subscription or a monthly contribution payment.
