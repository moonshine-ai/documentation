# Introduction

Q: What's the Bookshelf app on my Raspberry Pi for?
A: It gives you access to books and magazines from Raspberry Pi Press.

Q: I've got the Lite version of Raspberry Pi OS. Does Bookshelf come with that?
A: No, Bookshelf is only included in Desktop and Full images.

Q: There's a padlock on some of the titles in Bookshelf. What does that mean?
A: Those are contributor-only titles requiring a Raspberry Pi ID to access.

Q: I'm signed into my Raspberry Pi ID but I still can't open some books. Why?
A: Your ID must be linked to a contribution or print magazine subscription.

Q: How do I know which books I can download for free in Bookshelf?
A: Free titles are marked with a cloud icon and downloadable by anyone.

Q: When did Bookshelf start including contributor-only titles?
A: Contributor-only titles were added starting with Raspberry Pi OS Trixie.

Q: I don't want to pay for anything. Are there any free books in Bookshelf?
A: Yes, free titles marked with a cloud icon are available to anyone.

Q: Can locked contributor-only titles ever become free later on?
A: Yes, some contributor-only titles eventually become available to everyone.
