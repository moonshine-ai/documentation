# RP3A0

Q: What's the RP3A0 and what makes it different from a regular chip?
A: It's a System-in-Package combining a BCM2710A1 die with 512MB DRAM.

Q: Which Raspberry Pi actually uses the RP3A0?
A: The Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W uses the RP3A0.

Q: How many cores does the RP3A0 have and what speed does it run at?
A: It's a quad-core 64-bit Arm Cortex A53 clocked at 1 GHz.

Q: Can I overclock the RP3A0 and if so how fast can it go?
A: With a heat sink or cooling, it can potentially be overclocked to 1.2 GHz.

Q: What's the difference between how the Zero 2 W and the original Zero handle their RAM?
A: The original Zero uses Package-on-Package DRAM soldered directly on the BCM2835.

Q: I want to look up the peripherals for the RP3A0, where should I look?
A: Refer to the BCM2836 Arm-local peripherals document, which also covers the RP3A0.

Q: Is the RP3A0 related to anything used on the Raspberry Pi 3?
A: Yes, it uses the same BCM2710A1 silicon die found inside the Pi 3's BCM2837.

Q: How much RAM does the RP3A0 come with built in?
A: The RP3A0 includes 512 MB of DRAM within the package.

A: A SiP combines multiple components, here a processor die and DRAM, in one package.
