# BCM2711

Q: What devices use the BCM2711 chip?
A: The Pi 4 Model B, Compute Module 4, and Pi 400 use it.

Q: How much faster is the Pi 4 compared to the Pi 3B+?
A: The Pi 4 is about 50% faster than the Raspberry Pi 3B+.

Q: What speed does the BCM2711's CPU run at?
A: The Arm cores can run at up to 1.5 GHz.

Q: I heard the Pi 4 can use more RAM than older models — how much?
A: It can access up to 8 GB of LPDDR4-2400 SDRAM depending on model.

Q: What's the GPU situation on the BCM2711, like what can it do?
A: The VideoCore VI 3D runs at up to 500 MHz with OpenGL ES 3.0 graphics.

Q: Can the BCM2711 play 4K video or is that too much for it?
A: It supports H.265 4Kp60 decode and H.264 1080p60 decode.

Q: Why does the Pi 4 have faster USB than older Pis?
A: A PCIe link connects the USB 2 and USB 3 ports for faster input/output.

Q: Does the Pi 4 have built-in ethernet or does it need an adapter?
A: The BCM2711 has a natively attached Ethernet controller built right in.

Q: What's the deal with the heat on the Pi 4, does it overheat easily?
A: It uses heat spreading technology from BCM2837B0 for better thermal management.

Q: How many HDMI outputs does the BCM2711 support?
A: It supports dual HDMI video output.

Q: Is the CPU 64-bit on the Pi 4?
A: Yes, the Arm cores in the BCM2711 are 64-bit.
