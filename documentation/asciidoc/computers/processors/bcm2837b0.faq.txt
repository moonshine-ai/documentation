# BCM2837B0

Q: What Raspberry Pi models actually use the BCM2837B0 chip?
A: Later Pi 2B, later Pi 3B, Pi 3A+, 3B+, and Compute Module 3+.

Q: I've got a Pi 3B+ — how much faster is it than the original Pi 3?
A: The 3B+ and 3A+ are about 17% faster than the original Pi 3.

Q: What's the max speed the processor can run at in the BCM2837B0?
A: The Arm cores can run at up to 1.4 GHz on the BCM2837B0.

Q: Is the BCM2837B0 totally different from the BCM2837 or basically the same thing?
A: The architecture is identical; only the clock frequency rating is higher.

Q: Why does my Pi 3B+ seem to run cooler than my older Pi 3? Is there a hardware reason?
A: The BCM2837B0 includes a heat spreader for better thermal management.

Q: Does the BCM2837B0 have a 64-bit processor or 32-bit?
A: The Arm core is 64-bit, while the VideoCore IV is 32-bit.

Q: What speed does the VideoCore run at on this chip?
A: The VideoCore IV runs at 400 MHz on the BCM2837B0.

Q: Why does the Pi 3B+ handle higher clock speeds than the older Pi 3?
A: The heat spreader allows higher clock frequencies and better temperature control.
