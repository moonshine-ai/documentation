# `disable_camera_led`

Q: The red light on my camera keeps turning on when I record. How do I turn that off?
A: Set `disable_camera_led` to `1` in your configuration to disable it.

Q: I've got my camera pointed at a window and it's getting reflections from that little red LED. Can I disable it?
A: Yes, set `disable_camera_led` to `1` to prevent the LED from turning on.

Q: Does the camera LED turn on for both video and photos, or just one?
A: The red LED turns on when recording video or taking a still picture.

# `awb_auto_is_greyworld`

Q: I've got a NoIR camera and my colors look all wrong. Is there a setting I should change?
A: Set `awb_auto_is_greyworld` to `1` to use the greyworld algorithm for NoIR cameras.

Q: What even is the greyworld option and why would I need it?
A: It's an auto white balance algorithm needed for NoIR cameras to capture valid images.

Q: I removed the IR filter from my High Quality camera and now the colors are off. What do I do?
A: Set `awb_auto_is_greyworld` to `1` since the IR filter has been removed.

Q: My app doesn't have a greyworld setting built in but I'm using a NoIR camera. Am I just stuck?
A: No, set `awb_auto_is_greyworld` to `1` to enable greyworld through auto AWB mode instead.

# Whole Document

Q: I'm setting up a NoIR camera and I also want to hide that red recording light. Are there two separate settings for that?
A: Yes, use `disable_camera_led` for the LED and `awb_auto_is_greyworld` for NoIR color correction.

Q: Do these camera settings apply to all Raspberry Pi camera models?
A: The greyworld setting is specifically noted for NoIR cameras and High Quality cameras with IR filter removed.
