# Onboard analogue audio (3.5 mm jack)

Q: I'm getting a weird hissing sound from the headphone jack. What's causing that and can I fix it?
A: Dithering causes hiss; set disable_audio_dither to 1 to turn it off.

Q: What's the difference between audio_pwm_mode 1 and 2?
A: Mode 1 is low quality legacy audio; mode 2 is high quality and the default.

Q: I've got mode 2 audio enabled and my Pi seems sluggish. Could that be related?
A: Mode 2 uses more GPU compute resources and can interfere with some use cases.

Q: What's the default setting for audio_pwm_mode, do I need to change anything?
A: The default is mode 2, which gives high quality audio automatically.

# `audio_pwm_mode`

Q: Is there a way to get better quality sound out of the headphone jack?
A: Set audio_pwm_mode to 2 for high quality audio using an advanced modulation scheme.

Q: What's legacy audio mode on the Pi jack — should I be using that?
A: Legacy mode 1 gives low-quality analogue audio; mode 2 is the better default choice.

# `disable_audio_dither`

Q: What even is dithering? Do I need it turned on?
A: Dithering adds a small noise signal to audio; it's on by default for analogue output.

Q: The volume on my Pi is turned down low and there's a hiss. Is that a known thing?
A: Yes, dithering can cause audible hiss at low ALSA volume levels.

Q: How do I turn off that background hiss on the headphone jack?
A: Set disable_audio_dither to 1 in your config to disable dithering.

# `enable_audio_dither`

Q: I'm using higher quality audio samples and dithering seems to be off. Is that normal?
A: Yes, dithering is normally disabled when audio samples are larger than 16 bits.

Q: Can I force dithering on even with high bit depth audio?
A: Yes, set enable_audio_dither to 1 to force dithering for all bit depths.

# `pwm_sample_bits`

Q: Can I change the audio bit depth on the headphone jack output?
A: Yes, use pwm_sample_bits to adjust it; the default is 11.

Q: I set the bit depth really low and now there's no audio. What happened?
A: Bit depths below 8 result in nonfunctional audio due to PLL frequency being too low.

Q: Why would anyone lower the bit depth on their audio? What's the point?
A: It's mainly useful for demonstrating how bit depth affects quantisation noise.

# HDMI audio

Q: Does HDMI audio work automatically on the Pi or do I have to set it up?
A: HDMI audio output is enabled by default on all Raspberry Pi models with HDMI.

Q: I don't want any sound going through HDMI. How do I turn that off?
A: Append ,noaudio to the dtoverlay=vc4-kms-v3d line in /boot/firmware/config.txt.

Q: Where exactly do I put the noaudio setting to disable HDMI sound?
A: Add it to the dtoverlay=vc4-kms-v3d line in /boot/firmware/config.txt.

# Whole Document

Q: I want the best possible sound from the 3.5mm jack. What settings should I check?
A: Use audio_pwm_mode 2 and consider adjusting disable_audio_dither and pwm_sample_bits settings.

Q: I've got audio going out both HDMI and the headphone jack. Can I control them separately?
A: You can disable HDMI audio with noaudio; analogue jack has its own separate config options.

Q: There's hiss from the headphone jack but only when it's quiet. What's going on?
A: Default dithering can cause audible background hiss, especially at low ALSA volume levels.
