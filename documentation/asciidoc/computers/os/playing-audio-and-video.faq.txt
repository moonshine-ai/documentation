# VLC media player

Q: Does VLC come already installed on Raspberry Pi OS?
A: Yes, VLC media player comes pre-installed on Raspberry Pi OS.

Q: I just want to open a video without using the terminal. Can I do that?
A: Yes, double-click a file in the file manager to launch VLC automatically.

Q: My audio is coming out of HDMI but I want it through the headphone jack. How do I change that?
A: Right-click the speaker icon in the system tray and select an option.

# `vlc` CLI

Q: I just want the video to close automatically when it's done. Is there a flag for that or something?
A: Yes, add the `--play-and-exit` flag when running VLC from the command line.

Q: I just want the video to go fullscreen automatically. Is there a flag for that or something?
A: Yes, add the `--fullscreen` flag, which can also result in smoother playback.

Q: How do I download that Big Buck Bunny test clip to my Pi?
A: Run `wget --trust-server-names http://rpltd.co/big-buck-bunny` to download it.

# Use `cvlc` to play media without a GUI

Q: Can I run VLC without a desktop, like just from the command line with no window popping up?
A: Yes, use `cvlc` instead of `vlc` and the GUI won't be shown.

# Play audio and video on Raspberry Pi OS Lite

Q: Does VLC work on Pi Lite?
A: VLC isn't pre-installed on Lite, but you can install it manually.

Q: I installed the Lite version and there's no media player. What do I need to get one?
A: Install `vlc-bin` and `vlc-plugin-base` using apt with no-install-recommends flag.

Q: I'm on Raspberry Pi OS Lite. Can I play an MP3 from the command line?
A: Yes, after installing VLC packages, run `cvlc --play-and-exit` with your file.

# Specify an audio output device

Q: What even is ALSA? Do I need to know about it to change my audio output?
A: ALSA is the audio system used; pass `-A alsa` to VLC to select audio output devices.

Q: How do I force audio out of the headphone jack instead of HDMI?
A: Use `-A alsa --alsa-audio-device sysdefault:CARD=Headphones` when running cvlc.

Q: How do I see what audio devices I've got?
A: Run `aplay -L | grep sysdefault` to list all ALSA devices on your Raspberry Pi.

Q: I've got a Pi 4 with two HDMI ports. How do I pick which one the audio comes out of?
A: Use `sysdefault:CARD=vc4hdmi0` for HDMI0 or `sysdefault:CARD=vc4hdmi1` for HDMI1.

# Specify a video output device

Q: I've got a touchscreen connected and I want the video to play on that instead of HDMI. How do I do that?
A: Use `--drm-vout-display DSI-1` to send video output to the Raspberry Pi Touch Display.

Q: How do I find out what video outputs my Pi has?
A: Run `kmsprint | grep Connector` to list all DRM devices on your Raspberry Pi.

Q: I've got a Pi 4 with two HDMI ports. Which device name is HDMI0 and which is HDMI1?
A: HDMI-A-1 is HDMI0 and HDMI-A-2 is HDMI1 on Raspberry Pi 4.

# Specify both audio and video output devices

Q: Wait, can I play audio and video at the same time to different outputs?
A: Yes, you can combine audio and video output options in a single cvlc command.

Q: I want video on my touchscreen but audio out of the headphone jack. Is that possible?
A: Yes, combine `--drm-vout-display DSI-1` and `--alsa-audio-device sysdefault:CARD=Headphones` together.

# Improve stream playback performance

Q: I recorded video with my Pi camera and playback is stuttering in VLC. What can I do?
A: Wrap the H.264 stream in an MP4 container using ffmpeg to improve playback performance.

Q: How do I convert a raw H.264 file to MP4 using ffmpeg?
A: Run `ffmpeg -r 30 -i video.h264 -c:v copy video.mp4` to convert at 30fps.
