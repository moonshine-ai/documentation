# Troubleshooting

Q: My camera preview is really slow and choppy. How do I speed it up on my older Pi?
A: Enable Glamor in raspi-config under Advanced Options, then reboot.

Q: I'm getting some display driver error with my camera. What do I do?
A: Add a vc4-fkms-v3d or vc4-kms-v3d dtoverlay to boot config, then reboot.

Q: My Pi 3 is recording video but the preview looks totally corrupted. What's going on?
A: Pi 3 graphics only support up to 2048 pixels wide; larger images corrupt previews.

Q: I've got a Pi 4 and my camera preview looks broken when recording. Why's that?
A: Pi 4 graphics max out at 4096 pixels wide, causing corrupted previews beyond that.

Q: My preview window has this weird flickering on the desktop. Is there a fix?
A: Display tearing in a desktop environment is a known issue with no fix available.

Q: I think my camera ribbon cable might not be in right. What should I check?
A: Ensure the FFC is firmly seated, fully inserted, and contacts face the correct direction.

Q: Wait, can the camera cable accidentally go into the wrong port?
A: Yes, only the CSI port powers the camera, not the DSI port nearby.

Q: My camera just stopped working randomly. Could it be my power supply?
A: The camera adds 200-250mA draw; a weak power supply may prevent it from working.

Q: I've tried everything and my camera still doesn't work. What now?
A: Try posting on the Raspberry Pi forums for additional help and support.

Q: I'm using an extension connector between my camera and Pi. Anything I should check?
A: Verify connector ports are firmly seated, fully inserted, and contacts face correctly.

# Whole Document

Q: My camera module just isn't working at all. Where do I even start?
A: Check the cable, port, power supply, software updates, and Glamor settings first.

Q: Does updating my software help fix camera problems?
A: Yes, updating to the latest software is one of the recommended troubleshooting steps.
