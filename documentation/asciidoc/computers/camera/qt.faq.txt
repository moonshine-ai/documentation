# Use `libcamera` with Qt

Q: I'm trying to use libcamera with Qt and getting weird errors. What's going on?
A: Qt defines macros like `slot` and `emit` that conflict with libcamera symbols.

Q: How do I stop Qt from clashing with libcamera in my project?
A: Include libcamera files before any Qt header files whenever possible.

Q: I can't avoid mixing Qt and libcamera includes. What do I replace `signals:` with?
A: Replace `signals:` with `Q_SIGNALS:` to avoid the macro conflicts.

Q: What's the quick fix I can add at the top of my libcamera include files to stop the errors?
A: Add `#undef signals`, `#undef slots`, `#undef emit`, and `#undef foreach`.

Q: I'm using qmake for my project. How do I fix the Qt and libcamera keyword conflict?
A: Add `CONFIG += no_keywords` to your qmake project file.

Q: I'm using cmake instead of qmake. What do I add to fix the Qt keyword conflicts?
A: Add `SET(QT_NO_KEYWORDS ON)` to your cmake project.

Q: Does rpicam-apps support using Qt for the preview window?
A: Yes, rpicam-apps includes an option to use Qt for camera preview.

Q: Is this Qt and libcamera conflict only a Raspberry Pi problem or does it happen everywhere?
A: This conflict is common to all platforms using both Qt and libcamera.

Q: I need to use `emit` in my Qt app but libcamera keeps breaking it. What do I do?
A: Replace `emit` with `Q_EMIT` and `foreach` with `Q_FOREACH` in your files.
