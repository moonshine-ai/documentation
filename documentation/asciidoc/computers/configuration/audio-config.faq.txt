# Audio

Q: What audio outputs does the Raspberry Pi OS support?
A: Raspberry Pi OS supports HDMI, headphone jack, and USB audio outputs.

Q: My Pi isn't connected to a TV. Where does the sound come out by default?
A: Without HDMI, audio goes to the headphone jack or connected USB device.

Q: I've got a USB speaker plugged in but sound's still coming out of HDMI. What's going on?
A: By default, Raspberry Pi OS outputs audio to HDMI 1 when available.

# Change audio output

Q: My audio is coming out of HDMI but I want it through the headphone jack. How do I change that?
A: Right-click the volume icon in the system tray and select your output device.

Q: I don't have a desktop, just the command line. How do I switch audio output?
A: Run sudo raspi-config, go to System options, then select the Audio option.

Q: I see this "Pro Audio" option in my audio settings. What even is that?
A: Pro Audio exposes maximum channels across devices for fine-tuned signal routing control.

Q: Should I use the Pro Audio profile or just leave it alone?
A: Unless you need fine-tuned audio routing control, use a different device profile instead.

Q: Where can I find out more about that Pro Audio profile thing?
A: More information is available on PipeWire's FAQ page on GitLab.

Q: I'm trying to change audio through raspi-config but I'm lost. What are the steps?
A: Go to System options, select Audio, pick your mode, then select Finish to exit.

Q: I right-clicked the volume icon but I'm not sure what I'm looking at. What does that screen do?
A: That screen is the audio output selector, letting you switch between audio output devices.
