# Connect a display to DISP1/DSI1

Q: I'm trying to connect a touch display to my Compute Module. What do I do first?
A: Disconnect the Compute Module from power before starting the connection process.

Q: Can I just use a Raspberry Pi Zero camera cable instead of the RPI-DISPLAY adapter to connect my display?
A: No, the two cables have distinct wiring and aren't interchangeable.

Q: I've got a CM4S and I'm wiring up the display to DISP1. Which GPIO pins do I need to jumper?
A: Connect GPIO pin zero to CD1_SDA and pin one to CD1_SCL.

Q: What do I add to config dot txt to get the seven inch touch display working on DISP1?
A: Add `dtoverlay=vc4-kms-dsi-7inch` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

Q: After I've done all the wiring and edited config dot txt, how do I actually get the display to start working?
A: Reboot your Compute Module using `sudo reboot` to detect the display.

Q: Do I need to update anything before I start attaching the touch display?
A: Yes, update your system software and firmware to the latest version first.

# Connect a display to DISP0/DSI0

Q: I'm using a CM3 and I want to use the DISP0 port instead of DISP1. Which GPIO pins do I jumper?
A: Connect GPIO pin 28 to CD0_SDA and pin 29 to CD0_SCL.

Q: What's the config dot txt line for connecting a touch display to DISP0 instead of DISP1?
A: Add `dtoverlay=vc4-kms-dsi-7inch,dsi0` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

Q: Does the CM4 IO board use jumper cables on specific GPIO pins for DISP0 like the CM3 does?
A: No, for CM4 add jumpers to J6 as indicated on the IO board silkscreen.

# Disable touchscreen

Q: I want to use just the screen part of my touch display without the touch part. How do I turn off touch?
A: Add `disable_touchscreen=1` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

Q: Do I need to do any special setup to get the touchscreen working once it's connected?
A: No, the touchscreen requires no additional configuration once connected.

# Disable display

Q: I've got a display plugged in but I want the Compute Module to just ignore it completely. How do I do that?
A: Add `ignore_lcd=1` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

# Attaching the Touch Display 2 LCD panel

Q: I've got the Touch Display 2 and I'm not sure if it connects differently from the original. Does it?
A: Connection is the same, but software setup differs because it uses a different display driver.

Q: What do I put in config dot txt to enable the five inch Touch Display 2 on DISP1?
A: Add `dtoverlay=vc4-kms-dsi-ili9881-5inch` to `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

Q: I want to use my seven inch Touch Display 2 on the DISP0 port. What's the overlay line I need?
A: Use `dtoverlay=vc4-kms-dsi-ili9881-7inch,dsi0` in `/boot/firmware/config.txt`.

Q: Does Touch Display 2 come in different sizes or is it just one size?
A: It's available in two sizes, either five inches or seven inches diagonally.
