# Power button

Q: Do I need to press the power button to start my Raspberry Pi 5 the first time?
A: No, it automatically turns on and boots when you plug it in.

Q: I pressed the power button on my Pi 5 and a window popped up. What is that?
A: It's asking whether you want to shutdown, reboot, or logout.

Q: How do I properly shut down my Pi 5 without just yanking the power?
A: Briefly press the power button and select shutdown from the window that appears.

Q: I've got the desktop version running. How do I quickly shut it down with the power button?
A: Press the power button twice quickly to shut down from the desktop.

Q: I'm running the Lite version without a desktop. How do I shut it down with the button?
A: Press the power button a single time to initiate a shutdown.

# Restart

Q: My Pi is off but still plugged in. Can I turn it back on with the power button?
A: Yes, pressing the power button restarts a powered-off but connected board.

Q: I just connected a HAT and now my Pi restarted on its own. What happened?
A: Connecting a HAT can reset the PMIC, which restarts the board.

Q: Is it safe to connect a HAT while the Pi is still plugged in?
A: No, always disconnect from the power supply before connecting a HAT.

# Hard shutdown

Q: My Pi is completely frozen and won't respond. How do I force it off?
A: Press and hold the power button to force a hard shutdown.

# Add your own power button

Q: Can I add my own power button to my Raspberry Pi 5?
A: Yes, use the J2 jumper by bridging its two pads with a momentary switch.

Q: Where is that J2 jumper located on the Pi 5?
A: It's between the RTC battery connector and the board edge.

Q: What kind of switch do I need if I want to add my own power button?
A: You need a Normally Open momentary switch bridging the two J2 pads.

Q: If I wire up my own button to the J2 jumper, will it do the same thing as the built-in button?
A: Yes, briefly closing that switch performs the same actions as the onboard button.

# Whole Document

Q: Does any of this power button stuff work on older Pi models like the Pi 4?
A: No, this only applies to Raspberry Pi models that have a power button, like Pi 5.

Q: Wait, so there's no clean shutdown option if I'm on Lite without a desktop?
A: On Lite, a single power button press initiates a clean shutdown directly.
