# Product compliance and safety

Q: Has the Raspberry Pi been tested for safety and compliance?
A: Yes, all Raspberry Pi products have undergone extensive compliance testing.

Q: Where do I find compliance info for my Raspberry Pi?
A: Check the Product Information Portal at pip.raspberrypi.com for compliance details.

# Flammability rating

Q: Is the Raspberry Pi board fire resistant or anything like that?
A: The PCBs used in Raspberry Pi devices adhere to the UL94-V0 flammability rating.

Q: Does the whole Pi meet the UL94-V0 flammability standard or just the board?
A: The UL94-V0 flammability rating applies to the PCBs only, not the whole device.

# Raspberry Pi Compliance Support

Q: I'm building a product with a Raspberry Pi inside and I'm worried about compliance testing. Is there any help available?
A: The Compliance Support programme connects you with engineers who tested Raspberry Pi products themselves.

Q: Who actually does the compliance testing if I use that support programme?
A: A dedicated team at UL assesses and tests your product through the programme.

Q: I don't really know how to handle compliance for my Pi-based product. Where do I even start?
A: Look into the Raspberry Pi Compliance Support Programme at raspberrypi.com for industry.

# Powered by Raspberry Pi

Q: I want to put the Raspberry Pi logo on my product. Am I allowed to do that?
A: You can apply through the Powered by Raspberry Pi program online for logo usage.

Q: My product uses a Raspberry Pi inside it. Can I say it's "powered by Raspberry Pi"?
A: Yes, the programme covers products with Raspberry Pi computers or silicon inside them.

# Approved Design Partners

Q: I need help designing something with Raspberry Pi but I don't know where to find someone.
A: Raspberry Pi has a list of Approved Design Partners offering hardware, software, and mechanical services.

Q: Are there consultants Raspberry Pi actually recommends for design work?
A: Yes, Approved Design Partners are consultancies Raspberry Pi works closely with and supports.
