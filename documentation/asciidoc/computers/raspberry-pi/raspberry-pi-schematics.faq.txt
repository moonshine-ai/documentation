# Whole Document

Q: Where can I find the official schematics and mechanical drawings for my Pi?
A: All schematics and drawings are available at pip.raspberrypi.com for each model.

# Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W

Q: Where can I find the test pad positions for the Pi Zero 2 W?
A: Test pad position documents for the Pi Zero 2 W are available at pip.raspberrypi.com.

Q: What's the RUN test pad for on the Pi Zero 2 W and where is it?
A: The RUN pad resets the board when connected to GND, located at 8.37, 22.69 mm.

Q: I want to check the Bluetooth is powered on my Zero 2 W. Is there a test pad for that?
A: Yes, the BT_ON test pad at 25.13, 19.55 mm shows Bluetooth power status.

Q: What does the STATUS_LED test pad do on the Pi Zero 2 W?
A: It shows the LED power state; LOW means the LED is on.

Q: How do I know which test pad is the 5V input on the Zero 2 W?
A: There are two 5V input test pads, located at coordinates 8.75, 11.05 and 11.21, 6.3 mm.

Q: What's the WL_ON test pad for on the Zero 2 W?
A: The WL_ON test pad at 27.7, 19.2 mm indicates wireless LAN power status.
