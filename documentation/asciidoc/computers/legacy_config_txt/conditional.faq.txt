# The `[HDMI:*]` filter

Q: I've got a Pi 4 with two HDMI ports. How do I set different resolutions for each one?
A: Use HDMI conditional filters like `[HDMI:0]` and `[HDMI:1]` in config.txt.

Q: Does the HDMI filter thing work on older Pi models or just the Pi 4?
A: The HDMI conditional filter is for Raspberry Pi 4 only.

Q: I'm confused — what does the HDMI filter actually do in config.txt?
A: It limits subsequent HDMI config settings to apply to one specific port.

Q: Wait, so there are two ways to write HDMI port settings? What's the other way besides the bracket filter?
A: You can use `variable:index` syntax, like `hdmi_group:0=2`, on port-specific commands.

Q: I set hdmi_mode but it's affecting both screens. How do I make it only apply to one?
A: Wrap the setting under `[HDMI:0]` or `[HDMI:1]` to target a specific port.

Q: Are those two syntaxes for HDMI settings actually equivalent or do they behave differently?
A: They're equivalent; both syntaxes produce the same result for port-specific HDMI settings.
