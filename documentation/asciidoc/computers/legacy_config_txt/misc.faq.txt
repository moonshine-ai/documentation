# avoid_warnings

Q: Can I still use turbo mode even when I've got a low voltage warning?
A: Yes, setting avoid_warnings=2 allows turbo mode even with low voltage.

Q: I keep getting low-voltage warnings but I want full performance anyway. What do I set?
A: Set avoid_warnings=2 to enable turbo mode despite low-voltage conditions.

Q: What does avoid_warnings=2 actually do?
A: It allows turbo mode to run even when low-voltage is detected.

Q: What is the logging_level setting for, exactly?

Q: I want more detail in my VideoCore logs. What do I tweak?
